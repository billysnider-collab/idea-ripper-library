[Use when]

Teams downstream discover problems only after the upstream team has committed.

[Why it matters]

Fields found pockets of Ford each analyzing only their own data. Pooling it let downstream teams flag costs before designs were locked. Catching the problem late had meant redone work, extra cost and slipped timelines.
