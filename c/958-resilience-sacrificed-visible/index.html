Card #c958IR // #c958Synthesis

Nobody can see resilience until it's gone, so it gets traded for things people can see: stability, productivity, efficiency. The bill arrives all at once, in a crisis. > “Because resilience may not be obvious without a whole-system view, people often sacrifice resilience for stability, or for productivity, or for some other more immediately recognizable system property.”

— In our words · Source: Thinking in Systems (Donella H. Meadows)

[Use when]

Something was optimized and now it shatters under pressure. The resilience was spent before the shock ever arrived.

[Why it matters]

Every round of optimization that strips slack and backup capacity is a resilience sale. The crisis doesn't cause the brittleness. It just collects on it.
