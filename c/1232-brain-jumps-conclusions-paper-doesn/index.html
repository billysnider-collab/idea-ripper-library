Card #c1232IR // #c1232Synthesis

"Luhmann states as clearly as possible: it is not possible to think systematically without writing (Luhmann 1992, 53)."

— In our words · Source: How to Take Smart Notes (Sönke Ahrens)

[Use when]

You're about to make a decision you've only reasoned through in your head.

[Why it matters]

Ahrens pairs this with Kahneman's description of the brain as "a machine for jumping to conclusions." Writing an argument out makes its gaps visible in a way that thinking it through in your head does not.
