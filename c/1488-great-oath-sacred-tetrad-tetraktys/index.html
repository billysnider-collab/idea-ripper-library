[Why it matters]

I swear this by Pythagoras himself, who transmitted to our souls the sacred Tetraktys—the fourfold mathematical pattern that is the fountainhead of ever-flowing Nature. Out of reverence, Pythagoreans never spoke their founder's name aloud in oaths, referring to him instead as "the one who handed down to our soul the *tetraktys*, fount of ever-flowing nature (*pagan aenaou physeōs*)" (Greek, p. 120; note the Doric dialect *pagan* and *psychai*, marking this as an authentic archaic formula). The **Tetraktys** is the triangular figure of ten points arranged in four rows ($1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10$). Within early Pythagoreanism, this single figure contained the entire blueprint of the cosmos: - **Arithmetic:** The first four integers summing to the Decade (10), the base of all number. - **Harmonics:** The ratios of the musical fourth ($4:3$), fifth ($3:2$), octave ($2:1$), and double octave ($4:1$). - **Geometry/Cosmology:** The generation of physical space from Point (1), to Line (2), to Triangle/Plane (3), to Tetrahedron/Solid (4). It is the "fount of ever-flowing nature" because all changing physical phenomena are generated by these immutable numerical proportions. [INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC] In the Renaissance *prisca theologia* (notably Johannes Reuchlin's *De Arte Cabalistica* and Pico's *900 Theses*), the Pythagorean *Tetraktys* was identified directly with the Hebrew **Tetragrammaton** ($YHVH$), the four-letter unpronounceable Name of God. D'Olivet's addition of "model of the Gods" (*modèle des Dieux*, p. 119) reflects this esoteric lineage. For Renaissance Christian Cabalists and Platonists, Pythagoras had learned the mystery of the fourfold Divine Name from Moses or the Egyptians and encoded it geometrically as the Tetrad: Unity (1, the Father/Monad), Dyad (2, generative power), Triad (3, harmony/Spirit), and Quaternary (4, the manifested cosmos), returning to the Decade of perfection.
