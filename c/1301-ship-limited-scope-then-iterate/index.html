"“Previously, we were built for large-scale project implementations. We’d take three to five years and roll them out only when they were as close to perfect as we could get them." "We’ll take a limited scope, implement, and iterate. We move a lot faster.”"

— In our words · Source: Digital Impact (Steve Lucas)

[Use when]

Your team holds releases until they're nearly perfect and projects take years.

[Why it matters]

The Credit Union of Colorado switched from multi-year launches to small releases. It built integrations nearly five times faster, and its new digital member experience contributed to a 5% leap in NPS scores. Aiming for perfect at launch had mostly made launches late.
