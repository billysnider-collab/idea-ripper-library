Card #c497IR // #c497Synthesis

Since there is no such thing as a fixed state of balance, it is more useful to think of balancing, an ongoing process.

— In our words · Source: Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve (Stanley Rosenberg)

[Use when]

Someone treats a regulation practice as a one-time fix instead of ongoing hygiene.

[Why it matters]

The exercises aren't a fix, they're maintenance. "Social engagement is not a fixed state, nor should the position of C1 and C2 stay fixed after doing the Basic Exercise. These bones move the instant that our psychological state shifts in moments of happiness, satisfaction, fear, anger, or withdrawal." The nervous system is quick to upset and slow to settle — "although our nervous system is quick to be upset, it takes a longer time to settle down when we are safe again" — so health is the flexibility to return, not a state you achieve once. Repeat as needed, for life.
