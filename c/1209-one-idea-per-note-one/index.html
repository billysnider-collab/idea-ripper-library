"By restricting ourselves to one format, we also restrict ourselves to just one idea per note and force ourselves to be as precise and brief as possible." "A good rule of thumb for working digitally is: Each note should fit onto the screen and there should be no need of scrolling."

[Use when]

You're building a library of ideas, research or reusable material and want the pieces to combine later.

[Why it matters]

Luhmann used A6 slips. The size limit made every note a single unit that could be moved and recombined with others later. A note holding three ideas can only ever sit in one place.
