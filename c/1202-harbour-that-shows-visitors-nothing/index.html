Appian describes two harbours sharing one entrance closed with iron chains. Merchant ships used the rectangular port and passed into the city through gates. Warships sat in the circular port behind a double wall, 220 berths round an island, and the admiral's house on the island watched the sea while arriving ships saw nothing of the docks.
