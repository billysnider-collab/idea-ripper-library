Card #c851IR // #c851Synthesis
Distinguish love from being carried: if it never lets you stand still, choose a direction, or rest, it may be wind, not love. The feeling that carries you away is the tell.

— Source: The Divine Comedy (Dante Alighieri)

[Use when]

You're looking at a relationship, habit, or obsession that feels passionate but keeps you from self-direction, and you want to tell attachment from being swept along.

[Why it matters]

The second circle's punishment is exact: "The infernal hurricane that never rests / Hurtles the spirits onward in its rapine." The sinners are not resting together. They are blown — never able to stop, choose, or land. The passion that governed them is now shown for what it was: being governed. The contrapasso names the distinction love tries to blur. Love chooses. Being blown doesn't. Love can stand still. The wind can't.
