— Source: Northanger Abbey (Jane Austen)

[Use when]

Someone dismisses an entire form they cannot name a single work in. Ask for the exception; watch it collapse.

[Why it matters]

The loudest contempt for a medium comes from those who have never touched it: Thorpe despises all novels, exempts Radcliffe's as the one worth reading, and does not know Radcliffe wrote Udolpho. His exception proves he never looked.
