Card #c1009IR // #c1009SynthesisMilitary report

Organizations below the national level can't adequately assess the risks and consequences of offensive cyber operations because of inexperience — but a simulation framework that combines subject-matter expertise with explicit assessments of uncertainty lets inexperienced organizations assess cyber risk at a proficiency level equivalent to an experienced organization.

— In our words · Source: Quantifying risk for decentralized offensive cyber operations (Michael S. Klipstein)

[Use when]

Inexperience is a process problem, not a talent problem — a framework that accounts for uncertainty can substitute for years of exposure.

[Why it matters]

As authority for offensive cyber operations decentralizes, the binding constraint isn't tools but judgment — and this suggests a structured process can close the experience gap instead of leaving risk calls to heuristics and biases.
