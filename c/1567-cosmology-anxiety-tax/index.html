[Why it matters]

Studying natural science has zero intrinsic value; we only need to understand the physical mechanics of the cosmos because otherwise mythic fables about the sky and the underworld will poison our peace of mind. Against both Plato and Aristotle — who crowned theoretical contemplation (*theoria*) of the cosmos and the astronomical spheres as the highest, self-justifying end of human existence — Epicurus ruthlessly instrumentalizes natural science (*physiologia*, rendered by Yonge as "physiological studies"). In the Epicurean system, knowledge for its own sake is an empty distraction. Physics is strictly a defensive weapon against *mythos* ("the fables"). If a citizen constructs an impenetrable social fortress among men (Maxim 12) yet looks up at thunder, eclipses, or planetary motions and suspects they are directed by wrathful agency, or looks down and fears Tartarus, his *ataraxia* collapses. Therefore, the philosopher studies atomic mechanics solely to prove that celestial phenomena have non-intentional material causes. The instant an explanation liquidates mythic dread, inquiry has done its job and can stop. [INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC] This passage represents the exact inverse of the Chaldean-Platonic astral theology championed by Ficino and the Renaissance magi. For the *prisca theologia*, the "heavenly bodies" are living, ensouled intermediaries (*daimones* and planetary intelligences) stepping down divine influx into matter, and astronomical contemplation is the ladder of the soul's ascent. In Renaissance reception, Epicurus's refusal to grant intrinsic sanctity to the celestial spheres was read two ways: orthodox Platonists condemned it as blindness to the cosmic Choir, while early modern natural philosophers and anti-astrological polemicists (including Pico in his *Disputationes adversus astrologiam divinatricem*, who strategically deployed naturalistic explanations to break judicial astrology's fatalism) used this exact mechanism to liberate human agency from astral tyranny. Strip the stars of mythic intention, and the terrifying deterministic web of fate dissolves into harmless mechanics.
