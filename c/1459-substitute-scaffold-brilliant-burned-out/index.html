A gifted cynic who wasted his own life in self-disgust cannot be saved by career rewards, but can find peace by taking the fatal blow meant for an honest peer he once envied.

— In our words · Source: A Tale of Two Cities (Charles Dickens)

[Use when]

A brilliant, burned-out veteran sneers at a rising colleague during a layoff review. Let the veteran volunteer to take the fall and protect the junior's job.

[Why it matters]

Selling your best brains to a fraud rots your self-respect. No promotion will ever make a burned-out cynic love himself again. He only buys back his soul by paying the price for someone else.
