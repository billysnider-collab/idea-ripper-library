Card #c1018IR // #c1018Synthesis

"She asks one why he wants the window open: 'To get some fresh air.' She asks the other why he wants it closed: 'To avoid the draft.' After thinking a minute, she opens wide a window in the next room, bringing in fresh air without a draft."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

Two positions look irreconcilable and everyone is haggling over how far to open the same window.

[Why it matters]

Asking each side "why" turned open-versus-closed into air-versus-draft, and the answer was a third option nobody was arguing about. Borrowed by the authors from Mary Parker Follett.
