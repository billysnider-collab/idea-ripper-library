Card #c595IR // #c595Synthesis

The sacrificial fee is not flat. At the fee-giving, the sacrificer gives according to his ability. The most expensive ritual technology in the ancient world prices itself in proportion to means. (praśna 2)

— In our words · Source: The Hiranyakeshi Shrauta Sutra (Vedic tradition)

[Use when]

Use when setting dues for something meant to include everyone. Scale with ability and watch the participation problem dissolve.

[Why it matters]

Flat fees filter by wealth; proportional fees filter by commitment.
