But Catherine did not know her own advantages—did not know that a good-looking girl, with an affectionate heart and a very ignorant mind, cannot fail of attracting a clever young man, unless circumstances are particularly untoward.

— In our words · Source: Northanger Abbey (Jane Austen)

[Use when]

You notice an arrangement that needs one party to stay uninformed. Ask who the ignorance serves.

[Why it matters]

Austen's deadpan inversion: ignorance is an asset because it gives the clever man a stage. The system rewards the performance of teaching over the substance of knowing, and the ignorant party is the one being used.
