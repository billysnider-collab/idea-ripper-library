[Use when]

You want outside traders in your market and need them to trust it.

[Why it matters]

In Carthage's first treaty with Rome, as Polybius copied it, the Carthaginian state undertook that Roman traders would be paid what they were owed in its ports. The same text listed what Romans could not do in Carthaginian territory, and one headland, the Fair Promontory, marked where they could not sail.
