Card #c1425IR // #c1425Synthesis

Build the pitch around the one specific gap in the other person's life, not around your biggest feature.

— In our words · Source: Software (Rudy Rucker)

[Use when]

Writing the opening line of a sales email: name the customer's specific pain before mentioning any feature.

[Why it matters]

The boppers don't offer the old, sick Cobb Anderson money; they offer immortality: "We want to make you immortal, Dr. Anderson." A pitch lands when it names what the listener lacks, so be wary when a pitch to you is shaped around your own gap.
