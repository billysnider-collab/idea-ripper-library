Card #c778IR // #c778Synthesis

We say: Cogito ergo jaunteo. I think, therefore I jaunte.

— In our words · Source: The Stars My Destination (Alfred Bester)

[Use when]

You need a one-line operating principle for a world where willpower is literally power; or a slogan that fuses a philosophy reference with a speculative mechanism so the reader grasps both at once.

[Why it matters]

Bester's jaunting is teleportation stripped of technology and reduced to pure will: to think is to move. This reframes the entire novel's physics as a property of mind rather than machinery, which is why no institution can control it and no one can contain it. Every other mechanism in the book (Pyr E, space-jaunting) is a variation on this thesis that thought is the only real force.
