Card #c510IR // #c510Synthesis

"Reality + Dreams + Humor = Wisdom" — Lin's calculus. Dreams (imagination, aspiration, idealism) are the fuel of life; humor is the regulator that keeps them from curdling into fanaticism; reality is the ballast. Reality without dreams is mere animal existence; dreams without humor harden into fanaticism; dreams and humor without reality are empty fantasy; reality and humor without dreams are dead conservatism. Wisdom is the three held together.

— In our words · Source: The Importance of Living (Lin Yutang)

[Use when]

A cause, a pitch, or your own enthusiasm starts feeling airtight — look for the missing term before it costs you.

[Why it matters]

Every movement, plan, and person can be diagnosed by asking which term is missing. The zealot has dreams and certainty but no humor; the cynic has reality and humor but no dreams; the naif has dreams and humor but no reality. Check your own ratio before you trust your own convictions.
