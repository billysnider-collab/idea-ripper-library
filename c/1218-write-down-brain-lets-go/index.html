Card #c1218IR // #c1218Synthesis

"Open tasks tend to occupy our short-term memory – until they are done." "All we have to do is to write them down in a way that convinces us that it will be taken care of."

— In our words · Source: How to Take Smart Notes (Sönke Ahrens)

[Use when]

Unfinished tasks keep pulling at your attention while you try to focus on one thing.

[Why it matters]

Bluma Zeigarnik saw a waiter recall unpaid orders perfectly and forget them once paid. Her follow-up research showed that writing a task down in a trusted place frees the mind about as well as finishing it.
