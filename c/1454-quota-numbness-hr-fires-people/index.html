Card #c1454IR // #c1454Synthesis

When severe punishment becomes a high-volume daily schedule, it loses all power to scare people; staff treat human ruin as a math quota while bystanders turn it into dark jokes.

— In our words · Source: A Tale of Two Cities (Charles Dickens)

[Use when]

HR fires people every week and staff make memes about the walkouts. Stop using threats to motivate the team and fix the broken workflow.

[Why it matters]

Heavy penalties only shock people when they are rare. Used every day, they turn clerks into robots and victims into numbers. Innocent people get ground up just to clear the Friday backlog.
