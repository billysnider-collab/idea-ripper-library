[Why it matters]

As far as cosmic law permits, you will perceive that Nature operates by the exact same structural principles everywhere, freeing you from hoping for impossible things or missing what is right in front of you. The Greek line (p. 120) states: *Gnōsēi d', hēi themis esti, physin peri pantos homoiēn*—"You shall know, so far as is lawful (*themis*), that Nature in all things is similar/uniform (*homoiēn*)." This is the supreme scientific and philosophical insight of Pythagoreanism: **cosmic isomorphism**. Because the entire universe is constructed out of the ratios of the Tetraktys, the same structural laws govern musical strings, planetary orbits, biological bodies, and civic justice. What is the immediate psychological cash-value of knowing that Nature is uniform everywhere? The Greek explains: *hōste se mēt' aelpt' elpizein, mēte ti lēthein*—"so that you neither hope for things beyond hope (the impossible), nor does anything escape your notice." Understanding universal natural law cures two diseases at once: delusional wishful thinking ("vain desires") and blind ignorance of how things work. [INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC] Across the Hermetic and Renaissance Platonic tradition, this verse was celebrated as the Pythagorean formulation of the Emerald Tablet's axiom: *That which is below is like that which is above*. For Ficino, Pico, and Agrippa, "Nature alike in everything" is the law of universal correspondence (*sympatheia*). Because the Three Worlds (elemental, celestial, intellectual) are structurally homologous, the initiate who understands one level holds the key to all three. "So far as is lawful (*hēi themis esti*)" marks the boundary of cosmic permission: the magus never tries to violate Nature ("vain desires"), but works strictly within her invariant harmonic laws to produce wonders.
