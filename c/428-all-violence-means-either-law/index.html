All violence as a means is either law-making or law-preserving. If it lays claim to neither of these predicates, it forfeits all validity.

— In our words · Source: Critique of Violence (Walter Benjamin)

[Use when]

Classifying any state use of force, from airstrikes to arrests.

[Why it matters]

The essay's master distinction: every violence the state deploys is either founding a legal order or defending one — there is no third, neutral kind.
