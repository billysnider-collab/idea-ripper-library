"…since they were paid by the hour, the faster they went the less money they got, and finally telling him that if he went too fast the other boys would give him a licking."

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

You are setting up pay for work where you can't easily see the pace.

[Why it matters]

A twelve-year-old had worked out the incentive and the enforcement on his own. Hourly pay rewarded slowness, and the group policed anyone who went faster.
