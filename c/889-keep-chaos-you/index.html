Card #c889IR // #c889Synthesis

“I tell you: one must still have chaos in one, to give birth to a dancing star. I tell you: ye have still chaos in you. Alas! There cometh the time when man will no longer give birth to any star.”

— In our words · Source: Thus Spoke Zarathustra (Friedrich Nietzsche)

[Use when]

When everything runs smoothly and nothing surprising has happened in months — that is the warning, not the win.

[Why it matters]

Over-systematized operations produce only what the system already knows. The dancing star — anything genuinely new — is born from the unplanned, the unmeasured, the slightly feral part of the work. Kill all your chaos and you kill your future output with it.
