[Use when]

Managing co-founder disputes, long-term creative partnerships, or moments when you are tempted to scrap a valuable alliance over minor interpersonal friction. ---

[Why it matters]

Select friends solely on the basis of their virtue, learn from their example, and do not sever the bond over minor faults—pushing your tolerance to the limit, because human capability is forged only under the pressure of necessity. For the Pythagoreans, *philia* (friendship) is the social expression of numerical harmony (*harmonia*). Unlike blood relatives, who are assigned by fate, a friend is deliberately chosen based on *aretē* (excellence/virtue). Once chosen, the friend serves as an externalized mirror and corrective gyroscope ("Yield to his gentle counsels"). However, human nature is composite and prone to error; abandoning an alliance over a minor offense (*hamartados mikrēs*) destroys a high-value harmonic structure over transient noise. The qualification "If thou canst at least: for a most rigid law / Binds Power to Necessity" (*dynamis gar anankēs engythi naiei*—"Power dwells near Necessity") cashes out a core pre-Socratic mechanic: human capacity (*dynamis*) is not a static reserve. You only discover and expand what you are capable of enduring when you treat the preservation of virtue and friendship as an inescapable constraint (*anankē*). [INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC] Within the Renaissance esoteric reception (notably Ficino's *De Amore* and the Neoplatonic commentary of Hierocles that Ficino translated into Latin), Pythagorean friendship is an alchemical vessel for soul-merging: "friends hold all things in common" because their souls are tuned to the same planetary ray. The maxim that "Power dwells near Necessity" was read by Renaissance Platonists as the secret key to magical will. *Anankē* (Necessity) is the inflexible spindle of cosmic fate governing the material world; *Dynamis* (spiritual power) is awakened only when the initiate stops looking for easy exits and voluntarily submits to the friction of another soul. By enduring a brother's minor friction without breaking the circle, the initiate transmutes leaden social constraint into sovereign spiritual potency.
