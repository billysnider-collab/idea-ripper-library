"The other (and more important) thing I learned was that Barstool was driving the ticket sales; not the artists."

— In our words · Source: Cancel Me If You Can (Dave Portnoy)

[Use when]

You're paying a premium for an outside draw and haven't tested whether your own brand is the real pull.

[Why it matters]

Paying for far bigger acts sold exactly the same number of tickets as the cheap tour, and fans wanted photos with Portnoy. He swore off paying for outside talent. The next tour used in-house people and cleared about $200,000 a night.
