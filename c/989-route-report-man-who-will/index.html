Card #c989IR // #c989Synthesis

After reading the Vrba-Wetzler report on Auschwitz, Dulles performed shock: “One has to do something immediately.” Then he sent a routine cable to Cordell Hull, “a man Dulles knew would not lift a finger.” To kill an urgent report without refusing it, route it to someone guaranteed to sit on it.

— In our words · Source: The Devil's Chessboard (David Talbot)

[Use when]

An alarming report gets an impressive immediate response followed by assignment to the one person sure to do nothing.

[Why it matters]

Refusal creates a record; routing creates deniability. The report was “handled.” Handled straight into a drawer.
