Card #c1136IR // #c1136Synthesis

"…only one workman at a time should be dealt with at the start. Until this single man has been thoroughly convinced that a great gain has come to him from the new method, no further change should be made." "…very rapid progress can be made, because at about this time there is, generally, a complete revolution in the public opinion of the whole establishment…"

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

You are rolling out a new way of working and want to announce it to everyone on day one.

[Why it matters]

Taylor put a full changeover at two to three years and sometimes four to five. It starts with one convinced worker, and once a quarter to a third of the workforce has switched, opinion turns and the rest ask to join.
