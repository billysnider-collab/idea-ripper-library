Card #c410IR // #c410SynthesisThesis

Shared lexical innovations between Indo-Iranian and Balto-Slavic reveal a period of shared development — the Indo-Slavic linkage — meaning Indo-Iranian did not spread straight eastward from the Core Indo-European homeland; it moved into northeast Europe during the 3rd millennium BCE and only then spread east toward the Urals.

— In our words · Source: Indo-Slavic lexical isoglosses and the prehistoric dispersal of Indo-Iranian (A.I. Palmér)

[Use when]

Shared vocabulary is a migration map — the words two language branches innovated together trace where their speakers once stood.

[Why it matters]

It redraws the migration map — a detour through northeast Europe instead of the long-assumed direct eastward push out of the steppe.
