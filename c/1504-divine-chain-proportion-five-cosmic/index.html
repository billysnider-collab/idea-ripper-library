According then to a fair reason he formed it of earth and fire. But since it was requisite for some chain to be in the midst of both of these; and since the divine chain is that of proportion, which has by nature the power to make itself and what are united with it one; and since the World was not a plane—for one middle power would have been sufficient—but spherical—and required two middle powers for the fitting together—on this account, in the midst of fire and of earth, both air and water were arranged according to the manner of a proportion; so that, as fire is to air, so air should be to water, and this last to earth, and conversely ; and by nothing being left from without, he made the World his only begotten... And he gave to them the form of a Pyramid, and a Cube, and an Octohedron, and Eikosihedron, but, above all, a Dodecahedron... And of the nature of a Plane there are, as it were, some two progenitors, the most beautiful, in the form of right-angled triangles; one, the Skalene; the other, the Isoskeles... These things, however, when separated, do not remain at rest, but have a ceaseless shaking and communicate it to Matter. Wherefore being bound to the circumference of the World, they are driven on with it; and, while so driven on, they are carried against each other, the thinner particles into the places of the grosser; and by this means there is left no vacuum, destitute of some body... — Alcinous, *Handbook*, Chs. 12–13, trans. [Why it matters]

To bind the opposite elements of Fire and Earth into a solid spherical cosmos, God inserted two proportional middle terms—Air and Water ($F:A :: A:W :: W:E$). He built the four elements from two fundamental right triangles into four regular solids, using the 360-triangle Dodecahedron for the zodiacal heavens, while cosmic rotation compresses the particles to eliminate any vacuum. Stepping from Theology down into Physics, Alcinous explains how the Demiurge tames chaotic Matter (which previously held only restless "foot-marks" of the elements shaking without measure) into a single, ageless, spherical cosmos. First comes **macro-structural bonding**: a physical world must be visible (requiring Fire) and tangible/solid (requiring Earth). How do you bind two hostile extremes into an unbreakable unity? Through the "divine chain of proportion" (*analogia*). In geometry, two square numbers (planes) require only one mean proportional between them (e.g., $4 : 6 : 9$), whereas two cube numbers (solids) require *two* mean proportionals (e.g., $8 : 12 : 18 : 27$). Because the cosmos is a three-dimensional solid sphere, the deity inserted *two* middle elements—Air and Water—locking the four elements into a continuous geometric ratio. Furthermore, by using up *all* existing Fire, Air, Water, and Earth with zero leftovers outside the sphere, the world is immune to external disease or assault. Second comes **micro-structural geometry**: solids are bounded by planes, and all elemental planes are generated from two "progenitor" right-angled triangles: the half-equilateral *Skalene* (which builds the sharp 4-faced Pyramid of Fire, the 8-faced Octahedron of Air, and the 20-faced Icosahedron of Water) and the *Isosceles* (which builds the stable 6-faced Cube of Earth). The 12-pentagon Dodecahedron, subdividing into 360 triangles, maps the 12 signs and 360 degrees of the Zodiac. Bound inside the rotating outer sphere, smaller, sharper pyramids slice through and pack into the gaps between larger cubes, eliminating any void and sustaining perpetual physical circulation. **[INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC]** In the Renaissance *prisca theologia* tradition—stretching from Ficino’s *Timaeus* commentary to Johannes Kepler’s *Mysterium Cosmographicum*—Chapters 12 and 13 of Alcinous provide the mathematical cipher of creation. Ficino (as cited in Burges’s 1854 notes) expands the proportional chain to show its full reciprocal circularity from Fire down to Earth and from Earth back up to Fire. For the esotericist, the "divine chain of proportion" is the law of cosmic sympathy: two distant, incompatible realms (such as the pure immaterial Intellect and the gross earthly body, or Fire and Earth) can never be joined directly; in a three-dimensional reality, they require *two* proportional mediators (such as World-Soul and Astral Spirit, or Air and Water) that share a continuous ratio. Moreover, Alcinous’s explicit calculation that the 12 pentagons of the Dodecahedron divide into 60 isosceles triangles and 300 scalene sub-triangles—totaling **360 triangles** corresponding to the 12 zodiacal animals of 30 degrees each—was seized upon by Renaissance astrologers and geometers as proof that the Zodiac is not an arbitrary human star-chart, but the literal geometric scaffolding stamped onto the heavens by the Divine Architect.
