Let us not desert one another; we are an injured body.

— Source: Northanger Abbey (Jane Austen)

[Use when]

Use when your field is under attack from outside. Close ranks; the reviewers will not save you.

[Why it matters]

Six words of maker solidarity: the producers of the despised form owe each other loyalty precisely because the world withholds it. No one else will patronize the work, so the workers must.
