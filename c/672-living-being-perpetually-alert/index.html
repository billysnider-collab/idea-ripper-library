Card #c672IR // #c672Synthesis

Alertness is not a technique; it's the default condition of a living thing. 'For him living is being perpetually alert for the hunter, who sometimes does not exist.' The creature's whole existence is shaped by continuous vigilance — not occasional vigilance, not vigilance-when-threatened. Presence is that baseline state of being ready for what may come, held continuously rather than summoned on demand.

— In our words · Source: Meditations on Hunting (José Ortega y Gasset)

[Use when]

You treat focus as something to turn on for meetings or deadlines — it's a way of living, not an event. Use to notice how much of your life is spent un-alert.

[Why it matters]

Ortega's observation is about the prey, but it inverts cleanly: the animal's life is 'shaped by the incessant wait for an aggression.' Vigilance as a permanent state, not a response. It's the sharpest statement in the book that attention is existential, not episodic.
