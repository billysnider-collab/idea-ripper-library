[Use when]

Good ideas show up when you aren't working and are gone by the time you are.

[Why it matters]

Bacon, Eldon, Pye Smith and John Hunter all kept running notes, and the habit gave them stock to draw on later. Pye Smith's biographer called him "always at work, always in advance, always accumulating."
