"What we are all looking for, however, is the readymade, competent man; the man whom some one else has trained."

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

A role has been open for months because nobody fully qualified applies.

[Why it matters]

Taylor says demand for competent people always runs ahead of supply because everyone is shopping for the finished product. His alternative is to systematically train the competent hire yourself.
