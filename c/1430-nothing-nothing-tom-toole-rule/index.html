Card #c1430IR // #c1430Synthesis

Price every free offer by finding where the cost will be collected before you accept it.

— In our words · Source: Fearful Pleasures (A. E. Coppard)

[Use when]

A vendor offers a free tier or a free migration: find the lock-in, data-rights or renewal clause before you sign.

[Why it matters]

Tom Toole, sixty and chasing his lost youth, holds that it is "seldom possible to get something in return for quite nothing," and that even when you do, the danger matches the delight. Something-for-nothing is a loan against undisclosed collateral.
