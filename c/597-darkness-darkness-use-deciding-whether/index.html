Card #c597IR // #c597Synthesis

The ignorant man who initiates a pupil, and the pupil who gets initiated while ignorant — both go from darkness into darkness. Bad teaching is framed as a two-way injury, stated as cosmology in the section on who may teach. (praśna 26)

— In our words · Source: The Hiranyakeshi Shrauta Sutra (Vedic tradition)

[Use when]

Deciding whether someone is ready to teach — or be taught. If either answer is no, wait.

[Why it matters]

Any credentialing system that punishes only the failed student while ignoring the unqualified teacher has the liability backwards.
