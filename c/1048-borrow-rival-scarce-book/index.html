"He that has once done you a kindness will be more ready to do you another, than he whom you yourself have obliged."

— In our words · Source: The Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin (Benjamin Franklin)

[Use when]

Someone with influence over you dislikes you and doing them favors hasn't helped.

[Why it matters]

A new Assembly member had spoken against him. Franklin asked a small favor instead of offering one, returned the book in a week with thanks, and says they "became great friends." The outcome is his account.
