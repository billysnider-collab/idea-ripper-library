"But local authorities in Cyprus and Algeria negotiated incessantly over things like permission for the plane to land, requests for additional fuel, access to news media, and deliveries of food. For each transaction these authorities successfully obtained the release of more hostages."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

You must deal with someone whose core demand you can never meet.

[Why it matters]

During the Kuwait Airways 422 hijacking, Kuwait never retreated on the core principle while every small practical matter was traded one at a time, each for more hostages. Hold the principle fixed; negotiate everything around it.
