Card #c1466IR // #c1466Synthesis

government even in its best state is but a necessary evil; in its worst state an intolerable one

— In our words · Source: Common Sense (Thomas Paine)

[Use when]

A process keeps growing because it feels responsible. Ask what concrete harm each step prevents, and cut the steps with no answer.

[Why it matters]

Even good government earns its keep only by the harm it prevents. Judge any rule by the evil it stops, not the virtue it performs.
