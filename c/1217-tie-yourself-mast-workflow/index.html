"With a good system, the mere necessities of the workflow will force us to act more virtuously without actually having to become more virtuous."

— In our words · Source: How to Take Smart Notes (Sönke Ahrens)

[Use when]

You keep resolving to be more disciplined and want to design the process so discipline isn't required.

[Why it matters]

Odysseus didn't rely on willpower against the Sirens. Ahrens applies the same move to confirmation bias: if the system has you collect every relevant note regardless of which side it supports, good habits come from the process.
