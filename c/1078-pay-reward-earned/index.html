Card #c1078IR // #c1078Synthesis

"the same gift which would afterwards be merely a reward is beforehand an obligation." "with men of lower stamp the honorarium paid beforehand acts rather as a bit than as a spur."

— In our words · Source: The Art of Worldly Wisdom (Baltasar Gracián)

[Use when]

You plan to reward a partner after delivery and wonder whether to give it now instead.

[Why it matters]

The same favor changes meaning with timing. Given afterward it settles a debt; given in advance it creates one. Gracián limits it to people who feel obligation.
