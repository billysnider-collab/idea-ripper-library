Card #c1086IR // #c1086Synthesis
"Hope is a great falsifier of truth ; let skill guard against this by ensuring that fruition exceeds desire." "A few creditable attempts at the beginning are sufficient to arouse curiosity without pledging one to the final object."

— Source: The Art of Worldly Wisdom (Baltasar Gracián)

[Use when]

You're about to announce a launch, a hire or a product with big claims attached.

[Why it matters]

Gracián says the real can never match the imagined, and disappointed people turn critical. His fix is to let early results build curiosity without promising the finished thing.
