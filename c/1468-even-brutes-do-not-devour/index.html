Even brutes do not devour their young, nor savages make war upon their families

— In our words · Source: Common Sense (Thomas Paine)

[Use when]

A bigger partner harms you and calls it family loyalty. Judge the act itself, and let the claimed bond raise the standard.

[Why it matters]

Calling a power your parent does not excuse its violence. By the standards of animals, an attacker who claims kinship looks worse, not better.
