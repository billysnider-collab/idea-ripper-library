"At Camp David, President Sadat of Egypt and Prime Minister Begin of Israel agreed to a plan that would return the Sinai to complete Egyptian sovereignty and, by demilitarizing large areas, would still assure Israeli security. The Egyptian flag would fly everywhere, but Egyptian tanks would be nowhere near Israel."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

Both sides claim the same thing but actually want different things from it.

[Why it matters]

The maps dividing the Sinai failed every time. Splitting sovereignty from security gave Egypt the flag (its interest) and Israel demilitarization (its interest) instead of splitting the territory.
