Metals, we must confess, cannot be multiplied by the instinct and labour of Nature only; yet we may affirm that the multiplying virtue is hid in their depths, and manifested itself by the help of Art: In this Work, Nature standeth in need of the aid of Art; and both do make a perfect whole.

— In our words · Source: Hermetic Arcanum (Jean d'Espagnet (Collectanea Hermetica Vol. I))

[Use when]

When arguing that craft does not invent capacity, it surfaces what was buried.

[Why it matters]

Potential is latent in Nature; Art is the reveal operation — neither works alone.
