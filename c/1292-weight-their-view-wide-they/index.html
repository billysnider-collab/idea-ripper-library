"All other things equal, you should think that Jo only has 40% (0.20/0.50) of the confidence in her fair value as you do in yours."

— In our words · Source: Trade Outside the Box (Brent Donnelly)

[Use when]

The other side's number differs from yours and you have to decide how much to move.

[Why it matters]

Donnelly quotes Agustin Lebron's example from The Laws of Trading. Your rival's price is 0.50 wide and yours is 0.20, so blend the two fair values weighted by confidence. You end up with a better estimate than blindly trusting either one.
