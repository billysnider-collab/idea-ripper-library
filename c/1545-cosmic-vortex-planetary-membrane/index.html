“That many bodies, of various kinds and shapes, are borne by amputation from the infinite, into a vast vacuum; and then, they being collected together, produce one vortex; according to which they, dashing against one another, and whirling about in every direction, are separated in such a way that like attaches itself to like. But as they are all of equal weight, when by reason of their number they are no longer able to whirl about, the thin ones depart into the outer vacuum, as if they bounded through, and the others remain behind, and becoming entangled with one another, run together, and produce a sort of spherical shaped figure. This subsists as a kind of membrane; containing within itself bodies of every kind; and as these are whirled about so as to revolve according to the resistance of the centre, the circumambient membrane becomes thin, since bodies are without ceasing, uniting according to the impulse given by the vortex; and in this way the earth is produced, since these bodies which have once been brought to the centre remain there.” — Diogenes Laertius, *Lives of Eminent Philosophers*, Book IX, *Life of Leucippus*, III (trans. [Why it matters]

Worlds form mechanically when a swarm of atoms cuts loose from the infinite into empty space, forms a whirlpool that sorts shapes like-to-like, and weaves an outer spherical membrane that traps a dense core at the centre. To answer the Eleatic freeze of Parmenides and Zeno (who argued that without void there can be no motion or plurality), Leucippus posits two equally real principles: the plenum (indivisible bodies/atoms of varied shapes) and the vacuum (void). Diogenes preserves here the detailed cosmogonic mechanics of early Atomism. A swarm of atoms is severed ("borne by amputation," *apotomē*) from the infinite reservoir into a great void. Their collisions initiate a rotary whirl—the "vortex" (*dinē*). Without any designing mind or god, the vortex acts as a purely mechanical centrifuge and sieve: "like attaches itself to like" by size and shape. Fine atoms are squeezed out into the outer void; hooked and larger atoms entangle to form a spherical "circumambient membrane" (*chitōn / hymēn*), a cosmic skin that encloses the system, concentrates heavy matter at the resisting centre as earth, and ignites outer moist-clay compounds into stars by rotational friction. **[INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC]** In late antique and Renaissance reception, Leucippus' "circumambient membrane" and blind vortex stood as the great atheistic counter-myth to the Platonic *Timaeus*, yet esoteric readers repeatedly raided its imagery. While Ficino condemned the Atomists for replacing divine Providence with mechanical necessity (*anankē*), Renaissance natural philosophers (such as Giordano Bruno, who fused Lucretian-Democritean infinite worlds with Hermetic vitalism) reinterpreted the "amputation from the infinite" and the "membrane" as the organic birth of innumerable living worlds (*mundi*) within the infinite divine womb. The cosmic membrane (*hymēn*) echoed the Orphic World-Egg: a living embryonic sack spun out of the chaos of primary matter, separating an interior cosmos from the abyssal infinite while drawing nourishment from the exterior stream of corpuscles.
