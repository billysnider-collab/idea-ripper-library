Card #c836IR // #c836Synthesis

Pre-empt competition for a role you hold by framing it as pure cost — 'who will envy the one who stands first against the enemy's aim?' — so challengers conclude there is nothing worth seizing.

— In our words · Source: Paradise Lost (John Milton)

[Use when]

You hold (or are watching someone hold) a position of power and want to disarm rivals — or you're noticing a leader describe their own job as all sacrifice to make ambition look foolish.

[Why it matters]

Satan has just been praised as leader, and his first concern is not strategy but competition: in Heaven the throne "might draw envy from each inferior." His defense is to redefine the throne as a liability — the one who sits highest stands foremost against the Thunderer's aim, exposed to the greatest share of pain. If the prize is a burden, only a fool reaches for it. Ambition is made to look irrational while he quietly keeps the decision rights that matter.
