The consistency of your trading hours has a direct impact on the consistency of your results.

— In our words · Source: TradeBook: How to Build a Complete Trading System (Stock Market Trading and Investing) (Crede Sheehy-Kelly & Andrew Aziz)

[Use when]

When your trading day has no edges and neither do your results.

[Why it matters]

A one-line case for routine as infrastructure: concentrated volatility at defined hours lets a retail trader be sharp; 24/7 markets invite 24/7 bleed.
