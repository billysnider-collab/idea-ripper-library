Card #c444IR // #c444Synthesis

The Empire never ended. Early Christianity becomes a parallel information system: empire, persecution, hidden community, encoded knowledge, revelation, transformation. PKD overlays that structure onto modern America. The question: is VALIS actually a science-fiction story about information, and religion is PKD's ancient vocabulary for describing information?

— In our words · Source: VALIS (Philip K. Dick)

[Use when]

Ancient religious structures map exactly onto modern information systems: ask what the theology is compressing.

[Why it matters]

This may be the deepest rabbit hole in the book. Strip the theology and the structure is pure information theory: a signal persecuted by a noisy channel (the Empire), preserved by redundancy (the hidden community), transmitted in code (scripture), decoded by a receiver (Fat). The Empire never ended is then not a historical claim but a systems claim: the same information war is running now, with new hardware. PKD's mysticism may be a compression algorithm for something he could not say plainly.
