"How do you sell ads for a newspaper that doesn’t exist? On promises."

— Source: Cancel Me If You Can (Dave Portnoy)

[Use when]

You need paying customers before you have a finished product.

[Why it matters]

Portnoy pitched Hooters and gambling companies on one thing: his paper would reach men aged twenty-five to thirty-five, their exact customers. A tight audience promise was enough to bring in revenue before the first issue printed.
