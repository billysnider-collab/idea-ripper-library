Card #c1507IR // #c1507Synthesis

After placing in the face the light-enduring eyes, they enclosed in them the light-like portion of fire; which being smooth and dense, they conceived to be the brother of the light of day. Now this runs through the whole of the eye, and especially the middle of it, in the most easy manner, (as being) the most pure and clear; and having a sympathy with the light without, as like has with like, it furnishes the sense of sight. Hence when light has departed at night or become obscured, that, which flows from us, adheres no longer to the air that is near; but, being kept within, it smooths down and disperses the emotions within us, and becomes the bringer-on of sleep, by which the eyelids are closed; and when there is a great quietness, slumbers fall upon (us) with short dreams; but when some emotions are still left, frequent phantoms are produced around us; and in this way are formed visions, that become, according to a direct road, some, day-dreams, and some, night-dreams... — Alcinous, *Handbook of Platonism*, Ch. 18, trans. George Burges (1854), pp. 279–280. *Surface Gloss:* Sight occurs when pure internal fire flows through the pupil and fuses with kindred daylight. At night, this ray cannot bind to dark air; turning back inside the body, it either calms our internal motions into dreamless sleep or collides with leftover agitation to generate dreams. **The Attention-Conservation Loop:** The energy you use to scan and engage the outside world does not vanish when you shut the world out; it turns inward. If your internal state is calm when you disconnect, that redirected attention repairs and quiets your system. If you disconnect while still carrying unresolved daytime agitations, your own perceptive energy will collide with those leftovers and manufacture phantoms all night.

— In our words · Source: Handbook of Platonism (Alcinous)

[Use when]

Designing cognitive-hygiene protocols, explaining why high-output thinkers suffer vivid insomnia after sudden disconnection, or building a magic system where outward perception and inward hallucination run on the exact same fuel tank. ---

[Why it matters]

Alcinous summarizes the extramission-coalescence theory of vision from Plato's *Timaeus* (45b–46a). Fire has multiple grades: the fire inside the head is non-burning, smooth, and dense (*lampron kai pyknon*). Perception requires ontological kinship ("like to like"). During the day, the internal ocular stream exits the pupil and welds itself to external solar light, forming a single continuous, sympathetic body of light that transmits the resistance of external objects back to the soul. Notice the mechanical conservation of energy at nightfall: the ocular fire does not extinguish when the sun goes down. Because dark air lacks the kindred fire required for external bonding, the visual current is dammed up behind the closed eyelids. Forced backward into the somatic interior, this pure fire acts as a physical sedative, smoothing out the turbulent kinetic motions of the lower soul—unless residual daytime passions bounce the ray around inside, projecting internal phantoms (*phantasmata*). [INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC] In the Renaissance *prisca theologia* tradition, this passage is the physiological cornerstone for the occult theory of the *spiritus* and the power of the imagination (*vis imaginativa*). Marsilio Ficino—whose Latin translation Burges (1854) notes rendered the fire's nocturnal retreat as *sese ad interiora recipiens, demulceat atque resolvat* ("withdrawing into the interior, it soothes and loosens") and the waking/sleeping visions as *seu vere seu false* ("whether truly or falsely")—read the ocular ray as a subtle luminous vehicle. Because the eye projects an actual stream of subtle fire outward, an agitated or intensely focused soul can physically impress its internal phantoms onto the surrounding air or into the eyes of another person (the mechanics of fascination and the evil eye). Conversely, in contemplative withdrawal, shutting the bodily eyes redirects the solar fire of consciousness inward, illuminating the pneumatic mirror of the soul.
