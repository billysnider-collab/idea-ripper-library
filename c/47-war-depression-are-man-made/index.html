The hope of the twentieth century rests on its recognition that war and depression are man-made, and needless.

— In our words · Source: Tragedy and Hope (Carroll Quigley)

[Use when]

When catastrophe is framed as inevitable — ask who made it, and who benefits from the framing.

[Why it matters]

The entire 1,300-page tragedy compressed into the hope: if the catastrophes were made by human choices, they can be unmade by human choices — the book's title in one sentence.
