[Use when]

An offshoot has outgrown its parent and wants to keep the tie on its own terms.

[Why it matters]

Carthage sent an annual tithe to Melqart's temple at Tyre for six centuries, long after it outgrew Tyre. Malchus's son carried Sicilian war tribute there and finished the mission while his father was besieging Carthage.
