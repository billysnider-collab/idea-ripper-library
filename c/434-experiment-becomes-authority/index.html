Card #c434IR // #c434Synthesis

Mike begins as the ultimate outsider learning humanity from scratch, and ends as the man with the explanation. The dangerous transformation is not that he learns: it is that everyone else's willingness to project meaning onto him manufactures his authority before he has earned it.

— In our words · Source: Stranger in a Strange Land (Robert A. Heinlein)

[Use when]

Someone with exotic credentials, foreign, technical, or outsider, is being handed interpretive authority they have not demonstrated.

[Why it matters]

Charismatic authority is a demand-side phenomenon. The outsider does not seize the microphone; the audience hands it to him because they are desperate for someone to explain reality to them. Test every guru story against this: who needed the explanation first?
