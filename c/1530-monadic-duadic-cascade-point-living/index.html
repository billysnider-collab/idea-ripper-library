[Why it matters]

Quoting Alexander Polyhistor's *Pythagorean Notebooks*, Diogenes outlines a step-by-step cosmic derivation: the Monad generates the Indefinite Duad -> Numbers -> Points ("signs") -> Lines -> Planes -> Solids -> the four sensible elements -> a spherical cosmos pierced by a vivifying solar ray. Diogenes preserves here a famous fragment from the first-century BCE scholar Alexander Polyhistor, who transcribed Hellenistic *Pythagorean Notebooks* (*Hypomnemata*). Historically, this text represents a fusion of early Pythagorean number-cosmology (Philolaus's "finite and infinite" at VIII.85), Old Academic dimensional derivation (Plato's "unwritten doctrines" and Speusippus/Xenocrates), and Stoic vitalism (the warm *aither* piercing the cold air and dense moisture). Unlike dualistic systems where two co-equal gods war with each other, this is a **monistic derivation**: the Monad (unity/limit/cause) comes first; out of it proceeds the Indefinite Duad (*aoristos dyas*, multiplicity/matter/substrate) as a subordinate instrument. Their interaction generates a strict dimensional cascade: `Monad + Duad -> Numbers -> Points (semeia, Yonge's "signs") -> Lines (1D) -> Plane Figures (2D) -> Solid Polyhedra (3D) -> Sensible Elemental Bodies`. Once the geometric shell is built, a solar ray of warm *aither* penetrates the stagnant sublunary air ("cold aither") and sea ("dense aither"), imparting life to plants and animals and tearing off fragments of immortal *aither* to form souls. [INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC] In the Renaissance Kabbalistic-Pythagorean synthesis (Pico della Mirandola, Johannes Reuchlin’s *De Arte Cabalistica*, and John Dee’s *Monas Hieroglyphica*), Alexander Polyhistor's cascade is the master key to mathematical magic. Creation is not a carpenter hammering pre-existing wood; it is the self-unfolding of the Point (the Monad) through the Line, the Surface, and the Solid. Because sensible matter is simply crystallized geometry, and geometry is crystallized number, the magus who operates with pure number and proportion operates at the causal root above the four elements. Furthermore, the solar ray that pierces both the "cold ether" (atmosphere) and the "dense ether" (the abyssal waters) to quicken all seeds is identified by Ficino with the *spiritus mundi*—the vital solar quintessence that the philosopher draws down through music, clean air, and heliacal contemplation to nourish the etheric vehicle of the soul.
