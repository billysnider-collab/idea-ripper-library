Card #c492IR // #c492Synthesis

The common thread may be a fairly simple one: All of the problems in this list occur at least partly from dorsal vagal activity or activation of the spinal sympathetic nervous system, and can be addressed by reinstating normal function of the ventral vagus nerve branch and other nerves required for social engagement.

— In our words · Source: Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve (Stanley Rosenberg)

[Use when]

A stack of unrelated diagnoses keeps multiplying in the same patient.

[Why it matters]

The book's master argument against symptom-by-symptom medicine. The "Heads of the Hydra" list — migraines, anxiety, COPD, digestive trouble, insomnia, fibromyalgia — isn't dozens of diseases but one stuck autonomic state wearing different masks; the comorbidity is the clue, not a coincidence. "Rather than treating individual symptoms as separate issues with a pill to be taken for each one, it would be preferable to find a common thread that links them." Cut off heads one at a time and two grow back; reinstate ventral vagal function and the beast dies at the root. Stated as his clinical position from his own practice — he labels his cases anecdotal, not trial results.
