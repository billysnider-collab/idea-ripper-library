Card #c735IR // #c735Synthesis

I will not adopt that ungenerous and impolitic custom so common with novel-writers, of degrading by their contemptuous censure the very performances, to the number of which they are themselves adding—joining with their greatest enemies in bestowing the harshest epithets on such works.

— In our words · Source: Northanger Abbey (Jane Austen)

[Use when]

Use when tempted to punch down at your own form to look serious. Defend the thing you make.

[Why it matters]

Creators trash their own medium to buy seriousness from its enemies, and in doing so join the attack on their own work. Austen names the trade: contempt for your own craft is ungenerous and impolitic, and it never purchases the respect it is after.
