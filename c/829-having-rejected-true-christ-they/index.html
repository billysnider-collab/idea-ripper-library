Card #c829IR // #c829Synthesis

It was the judicial punishment of the Jews, as of all unbelievers in one way or another, that, having rejected the true Christ, they should take up with a false one.

— In our words · Source: The Patristical Idea of Antichrist (St. John Henry Newman)

[Use when]

Watching people who left a faith or a cause and then attached themselves to something worse. Use when the emptiness left by rejection keeps getting filled by impostors.

[Why it matters]

Newman's hardest doctrine in the lectures: unbelief does not leave you empty — it leaves you occupied. Rejection of the true is not a neutral vacancy; it is a judgment that takes the form of being handed over to the counterfeit. The mind that refuses the real Christ does not stay free; it becomes available to whoever comes in his own name.
