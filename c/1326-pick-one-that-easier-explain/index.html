[Use when]

Two options look close and you'll have to defend whichever you pick.

[Why it matters]

Joe Manchin gave this to Mark Kelly when he was a freshman senator. Kelly had spent a career on carriers and launch pads, where what counted was making good decisions and articulating them clearly. If you can't explain a choice to the people affected, that tells you something about the choice.
