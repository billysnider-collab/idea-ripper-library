Card #c1022IR // #c1022Synthesis

"In this difference lies the potential for a bargain advantageous to both sides. Risk can be traded for revenue."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

The negotiation looks zero-sum because both sides seem to want the same thing.

[Why it matters]

Deals exist because of differences, not agreements: the Law of the Sea priced seabed mining low while companies' risk was high and higher once risk fell. Hunt for what each side values differently and trade across it.
