[Why it matters]

Heraclitus attacks famous polymaths for mistaking massive information-gathering for intelligence, defining true wisdom as grasping the single governing principle of the cosmos, and locks his deliberately obscure treatise inside Artemis' temple to filter out casual readers. Within Diogenes' third-century compilation of Ionian doxography, Heraclitus sets *polymathiē* ("abundant learning"—the archival accumulation of genealogies, rites, and geographical facts practiced by Hesiod, Pythagoras, Xenophanes, and Hecataeus) against *noos* ("mind" or structural insight). Information volume cannot generate cognitive order. Wisdom (*sophia*) consists instead in grasping a single operational principle—what Yonge renders "that idea" (*gnōmē*), the immanent steering intelligence that pilots all things through all things. Because the cosmos is an interconnected system governed by one law of transformation, piling up disconnected particulars blinds the observer to the mechanism. The stylistic obscurity (*asapheia*) and the physical deposit of the scroll in the Ephesian temple of Diana (Artemis) function as a deliberate cognitive filter: the syntax mimics the hiddenness of nature's own steering law, forcing the reader to perform the same active decoding required to read the physical world. **[INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC]** In the Renaissance *prisca theologia* lineage consolidated by Marsilio Ficino and Giovanni Pico della Mirandola, Heraclitus' temple-deposit and deliberate obscurity became the classical warrant for esotericism as a protective discipline (*disciplina arcani*). Ficino read Heraclitus as a veiled theologian of the divine *Logos* whose "one idea" governing all things through all things was the cosmic World-Soul (*anima mundi*) or fiery vehicle of divine Mind. By placing his book in Diana's sanctuary and wrapping his physics in riddles, Heraclitus practiced what the Renaissance Platonists called "poetic theology": hiding pearls from the profane herd so that only initiates capable of contemplative ascent could unlock the text. The attack on Pythagoras here was smoothed over by later syncretists as a surface quarrel between two masters who both taught that discursive, encyclopedic reason must yield to unified noetic intuition.
