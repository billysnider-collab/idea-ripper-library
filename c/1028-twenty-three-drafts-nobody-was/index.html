"After thirteen days and some twenty-three drafts, the United States had a text it was prepared to recommend. When President Carter did recommend it, Israel and Egypt accepted."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

Two sides are locked into incompatible proposals and direct bargaining keeps failing.

[Why it matters]

The one-text procedure: one draft nobody owns, criticism instead of concessions, repeat. Each draft is easier to criticize than a position is to abandon, so positions soften without anyone losing face.
