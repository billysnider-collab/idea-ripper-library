Card #c133IR // #c133Synthesis

The tenth rule: The major premise for tactics is the development of operations that will maintain a constant pressure upon the opposition. It is this unceasing pressure that results in the reactions from the opposition that are essential for the success of the campaign.

— In our words · Source: Rules for Radicals (Saul D. Alinsky)

[Use when]

When you're designing a campaign as a sequence, not a single event.

[Why it matters]

The action is in the reaction — you don't win exchanges, you keep the enemy reacting, because reactions are what you steer.
