Card #c1121IR // #c1121Synthesis
"…when pig iron is being handled (each pig weighing 92 pounds), a first-class workman can only be under load 43 per cent of the day. He must be entirely free from load during 57 per cent of the day."

— Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

People are grinding without breaks on heavy work and output is falling.

[Why it matters]

Carl Barth plotted the data and found that how heavy the load was set how much of the day a man could be under it. Scheduled rest was what made the higher daily tonnage possible.
