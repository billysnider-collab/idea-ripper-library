Card #c1123IR // #c1123Synthesis

"…yellow paper showed the man that he had failed to do his full task the day before…" "…whenever the men received white slips they knew that everything was all right, and whenever they received yellow slips they realized that they must do better…"

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

People find out how they're doing only at an annual review.

[Why it matters]

Each morning every laborer got yesterday's result on a colored slip. Many couldn't read, and the color carried the message anyway. Daily, individual feedback replaced a foreman's general impression.
