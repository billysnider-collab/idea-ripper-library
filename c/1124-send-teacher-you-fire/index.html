Card #c1124IR // #c1124Synthesis

"…if the workman fails to do his task, some competent teacher should be sent to show him exactly how his work can best be done, to guide, help, and encourage him, and, at the same time, to study his possibilities as a workman."

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

Someone misses targets in their first month and the reflex is to let them go.

[Why it matters]

The first response to a miss was instruction. If that failed the man was moved to work he was better suited to, "instead of brutally discharging the man or lowering his wages."
