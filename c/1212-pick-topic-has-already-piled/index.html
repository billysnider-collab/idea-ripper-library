Card #c1212IR // #c1212Synthesis

"Do not brainstorm for a topic. Look into the slip-box instead to see where chains of notes have developed and ideas have been built up to clusters."

— In our words · Source: How to Take Smart Notes (Sönke Ahrens)

[Use when]

You need to choose your next project, essay or product idea and are staring at a blank page.

[Why it matters]

A topic chosen by brainstorming starts with nothing behind it. A cluster of notes shows where your interest and material already are, so the project starts half-researched.
