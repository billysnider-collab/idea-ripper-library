"For many people, all of politics—left, right, and center—is over there, whereas their real life is over here."

— Source: This Is the Plan (Ben Wikler)

[Use when]

You're pitching to people who don't follow your field and you're about to use your field's categories.

[Why it matters]

In the survey Wikler cites, the biggest group of swing voters described their views in kitchen-table terms like "affordable" and "cost of living." They let the news come to them rather than seeking it out. A left-right pitch misses them entirely.
