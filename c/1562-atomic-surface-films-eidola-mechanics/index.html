Moreover, there are images resembling, as far as their form goes, the solid bodies which we see, but which differ materially from them in the thinness of their substance. In fact it is not impossible but that there may be in space some secretions of this kind, and an aptitude to form surfaces without depth, and of an extreme thinness; or else that from the solids there may emanate some particles which preserve the connection, the disposition, and the motion which they had in the body. I give the name of images to these representations; and, indeed, their movement through the vacuum taking place, without meeting any obstacle or hindrance, perfects all imaginable extent in an inconceivable moment of time... Error and false judgments always depend upon the supposition that a preconceived idea will be confirmed, or at all events will not be overturned, by evidence. [Why it matters]

Speaking in his own voice to Herodotus, Epicurus explains that solid bodies continuously shed ultra-thin, hollow atomic films ("images") that travel at unimaginable speed into our eyes and minds; error arises only when an internal mental motion projects an unconfirmed assumption onto these incoming films. Here Epicurus speaks directly in his *Letter to Herodotus*, adapting Democritus’s atomism to solve the mechanics of远程 perception without recourse to immaterial forms or visual rays emitted by the eye. Every compound body is a vibrating lattice of atoms that continuously sheds one-atom-thick surface husks—**images (*eidola*, *typoi*)**—which retain the exact geometric arrangement and color-disposition of the parent solid, though their edges can be blunted or warped in transit through the air. Because these films have almost no depth or mass, they traverse the void at "inconceivable" speed, striking the sense-organs (or, if finer still, bypassing the senses to strike the mind directly in sleep) in a continuous cinematic stream that produces the illusion of a single stable object. Error occurs through a specific mechanical slip: the incoming *eidolon* triggers a secondary, internal atomic motion in the thinker—an **"initiation of the thoughts" (*epibole* / internal projection)**—which adds an unverified belief to the raw film before sensory testimony can confirm it. **[INTERPRETATION: PRISCA THEOLOGIA / ESOTERIC]** In the Renaissance occult and natural-magical reception (notably in Giordano Bruno’s art of memory and De la Porta’s natural magic, drawing on the Latin Diogenes and Lucretian atomism), Epicurus’s *eidola*—"surfaces without depth" continually剥off all bodies and streaming across space "simultaneous with the thought"—became the physical mechanism for telepathy, talismanic radiation, and the Evil Eye (*fascinatio*). If every object and living body constantly exhales coherent, ultra-swift husks of its own form and internal passion (just as Epicurus says in the next paragraph that voice-currents carry a "communication of sentiments" and "sympathy" from the speaker), then the cosmos is saturated with an invisible web of living skins. The magus who purifies the mirror of his *phantasia* from the "motion peculiar to the individual thought" can catch these streaming *simulacra* unaltered from afar, or intentionally project charged images from his own breast into the porous mind of a distant receiver.
