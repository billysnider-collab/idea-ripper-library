Card #c963IR // #c963Synthesis

Almost all attention goes to parameters, subsidies, tax rates, knob settings, which sit at the very bottom of the leverage ranking. Almost nobody works the top: goals, rules, information flows, paradigms. > “Probably 90—no 95, no 99 percent—of our attention goes to parameters, but there's not a lot of leverage in them.”

— In our words · Source: Thinking in Systems (Donella H. Meadows)

[Use when]

Everyone is fighting about the settings. Zoom out. The leverage is in the rules and the goals, not the knobs.

[Why it matters]

This is why public debate feels so furious and so useless. Enormous energy spent arguing over dials that barely move behavior, while the structure generating the behavior goes untouched.
