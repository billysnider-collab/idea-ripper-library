Card #c491IR // #c491Synthesis

All five of these nerves work together; if one is dysfunctional, the others will also be dysfunctional. If we improve the function of one, we also improve the function of the other four.

— In our words · Source: Accessing the Healing Power of the Vagus Nerve (Stanley Rosenberg)

[Use when]

Face, voice, hearing, and posture symptoms cluster together and get treated as separate problems.

[Why it matters]

Rosenberg's most practical extension of Porges: social engagement isn't a vagus trick, it's a five-cranial-nerve system — "the ventral branch of the vagus nerve (cranial nerve X), and pathways within cranial nerves V, VII, IX, and XI" — behaving as one coupled unit. The coupling is what makes his testing work: a trapezius squeeze testing CN XI reads out ventral vagal function, and fixing one nerve lifts the other four. One intervention point moves the whole system, which is why a throat test can predict facial-nerve function and a neck exercise can change a face.
