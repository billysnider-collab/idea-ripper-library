Against the prevailing assumption, Chinese cyber espionage is not the primary driver of PLA modernization — case studies show the majority of modernized PLA platforms were developed from overt acquisitions, indigenous research, and physical espionage; cyber theft works as a supplementary, not critical, component alongside those methods.

— In our words · Source: Chinese Cyber Espionage: A Complementary Method to Aid PLA Modernization (Jamie M. Ellis)

[Use when]

Question the load-bearing assumption — the loudest cause isn't always the main one.

[Why it matters]

It steelmans the contrarian case — theft headlines overstate cyber's role, and misdiagnosing the engine of PLA modernization misaims the policy response (export controls vs. counterintelligence vs. out-innovating).
