Card #c960IR // #c960Synthesis

When everyone pushes in different directions, the system stays exactly where nobody wants it, held there at enormous cost by all sides pushing at once. > “In a policy-resistant system with actors pulling in different directions, everyone has to put great effort into keeping the system where no one wants it to be.”

— In our words · Source: Thinking in Systems (Donella H. Meadows)

[Use when]

Massive effort produces zero movement and every faction blames the others.

[Why it matters]

This is the drug war, the obesity epidemic, the reform that changes nothing. The pushes cancel out. The system isn't stuck despite the effort. It's stuck because of it.
