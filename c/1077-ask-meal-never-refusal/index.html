Card #c1077IR // #c1077Synthesis
"Surprise them when in a pleasant mood, when a repast of body or soul has just left them refreshed" "It is no use applying when another has been refused, since the objection to a No has just been overcome."

— Source: The Art of Worldly Wisdom (Baltasar Gracián)

[Use when]

You need a yes from someone and can pick the moment to ask.

[Why it matters]

Timing gets three rules: ask when the person is in a good mood, avoid asking just after they refused someone else, and avoid asking after a loss. Having just said no once, they find the next no easy.
