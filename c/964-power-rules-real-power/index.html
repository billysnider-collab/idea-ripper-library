Card #c964IR // #c964Synthesis

Don't watch the players to find what's broken. Watch the rules, and who gets to write them. The people setting the rules decide what winning means. > “Power over the rules is real power.” “If you want to understand the deepest malfunctions of systems, pay attention to the rules and to who has power over them.”

— In our words · Source: Thinking in Systems (Donella H. Meadows)

[Use when]

You're diagnosing a broken system. Follow the rule-making authority, not the rule-following behavior.

[Why it matters]

Players optimize inside the rules. Rule-makers choose the game. Every rigged contest looks fair if you only watch the play.
