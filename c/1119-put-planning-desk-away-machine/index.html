"…it would be physically impossible for him to work at his machine and at a desk at the same time." "…under scientific management fully one-half of the problem is 'up to the management.'"

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

Your best makers are also doing all the scheduling and tooling decisions in their heads.

[Why it matters]

Under the old system the worker did the planning in his head while running the machine. Taylor moves it to a planning room and says the work splits better when "one type of man is needed to plan ahead and an entirely different type to execute the work."
