"Write exactly one note for each idea and write as if you were writing for someone else: Use full sentences, disclose your sources, make references and try to be as precise, clear and brief as possible."

— In our words · Source: How to Take Smart Notes (Sönke Ahrens)

[Use when]

You're capturing anything you expect to use in writing, a pitch or a decision months from now.

[Why it matters]

The stranger is your future self, who will have forgotten the context within weeks. Notes written in full sentences can be dropped straight into a draft. Keyword scraps have to be rethought from scratch.
