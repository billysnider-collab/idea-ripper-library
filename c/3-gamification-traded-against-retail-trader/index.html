A November 2024 study shed light on this trend, analyzing 142 gamification features in the mobile trading apps of 17 major U.S. brokers. The research found that while these features successfully increased retail trading activity and income for brokers and market makers, they often led to lower returns and higher volatility for the retail traders themselves.

— In our words · Source: TradeBook: How to Build a Complete Trading System (Stock Market Trading and Investing) (Crede Sheehy-Kelly & Andrew Aziz)

[Use when]

When a flashy app makes trading feel like a game and you trade more than planned.

[Why it matters]

The technology chapter's warning: the tools were built to increase broker income, and they work — by making you trade more and win less.
