"The most recent review of the available evidence found that a conversation with someone at their doorstep in a “high-salience” election like a presidential race results in a 3 percent boost in that person’s likelihood to vote. So, talk to thirty-three people and (on average) you’ll generate one vote."

— Source: This Is the Plan (Ben Wikler)

[Use when]

You're asking people to do slow, low-yield outreach and need them to understand what success looks like.

[Why it matters]

Wikler tells canvassers to set expectations low. About one door in five opens, and most conversations change nothing. Knowing the base rate keeps people going through dull hours, and shows that the effect is real.
