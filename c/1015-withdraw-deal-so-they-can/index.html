Card #c1015IR // #c1015Synthesis

"Terms negotiated between a major city and its Hispanic community on municipal jobs were unacceptable to the mayor — until the agreement was withdrawn and the mayor was allowed to announce the same terms as his own decision, carrying out a campaign promise."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

The other side agrees with the deal but cannot be seen agreeing with you.

[Why it matters]

The terms didn't change. What changed was who announced them and which past promise they fulfilled. Face is about reconciling the stand with past words.
