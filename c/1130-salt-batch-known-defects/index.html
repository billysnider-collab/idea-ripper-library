Card #c1130IR // #c1130Synthesis

"Every two or three days a lot of balls was especially prepared by the foreman, who counted out a definite number of perfect balls, and added a recorded number of defective balls of each kind."

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

You rely on reviewers or QA and have no way to measure what they miss.

[Why it matters]

Neither inspectors nor over-inspectors could tell the seeded lot from real ones, so each count could be scored against a known answer. Taylor says this removed "all temptation to slight their work or make false returns."
