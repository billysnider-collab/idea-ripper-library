"The Mexicans had a strong interest not only in getting a good price for their gas but also in being treated with respect and a sense of equality… Rather than sell their gas, the Mexican government began to burn it off, and any chance of agreement on a lower price became politically impossible."

— In our words · Source: Getting to Yes (Roger Fisher, William Ury & Bruce Patton)

[Use when]

You are tempted to use leverage to squeeze a better number and the relationship has to survive the deal.

[Why it matters]

The U.S. Secretary of Energy treated it as a fight over money and blocked a price already agreed. Mexico wanted respect and equality too. It burned the gas off, and a lower price became politically impossible.
