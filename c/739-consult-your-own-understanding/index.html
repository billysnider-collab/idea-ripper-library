What have you been judging from? Remember the country and the age in which we live. Remember that we are English, that we are Christians. Consult your own understanding, your own sense of the probable, your own observation of what is passing around you.

— In our words · Source: Northanger Abbey (Jane Austen)

[Use when]

Use when a suspicion you are nursing fails the base-rate test. Check the probable before the plot.

[Why it matters]

Henry Tilney's antidote to fiction-poisoned judgment is base rates: laws, manners, what is actually passing around you. A story that cannot survive contact with the probable was never evidence, only atmosphere.
