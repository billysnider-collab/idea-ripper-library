"'Method,' said the Reverend Richard Cecil, 'is like packing things in a box; a good packer will get in half as much again as a bad one.'" "'The shortest way to do many things is to do only one thing at once;'"

— Source: Self-Help (Samuel Smiles)

[Use when]

You have ten things open and none of them is getting done.

[Why it matters]

Cecil never left a task half done to return to later. De Witt's version was "One thing at a time": think of nothing else until the current dispatch is finished.
