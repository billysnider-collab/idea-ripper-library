The Mind therefore is not cut off, or divided from the essentiality of God, but united as the light of the sun.

— In our words · Source: The Divine Pymander (Hermes Trismegistus (tr. Everard))

[Use when]

Use when someone treats mind as a sealed private box cut off from the source.

[Why it matters]

Nous is continuous with God the way sunlight is continuous with the sun — division talk is category error.
