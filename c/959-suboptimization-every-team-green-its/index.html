Card #c959IR // #c959Synthesis

When each part wins locally, the whole can still lose. Departments hitting their targets while the organization fails looks strange until you name it: suboptimization. > “When a subsystem's goals dominate at the expense of the total system's goals, the resulting behavior is called suboptimization”

— In our words · Source: Thinking in Systems (Donella H. Meadows)

[Use when]

Every team is green on its dashboard and the company is red. Check whose goals are eating the whole's.

[Why it matters]

Every scorecard that rewards a subsystem for its own goals teaches that subsystem to eat the whole. The sum of local wins is often a global loss.
