"…it was found that a first-class man would do his biggest day's work with a shovel load of about 21 pounds." "…he would frequently go from shoveling ore, with a load of about 30 pounds per shovel, to handling rice coal, with a load on the same shovel of less than 4 pounds."

— In our words · Source: The Principles of Scientific Management (Frederick Winslow Taylor)

[Use when]

Everyone uses the same tool for every job because that's the tool they own.

[Why it matters]

One shovel per man meant he was overloaded on ore and nearly idle on coal. Bethlehem stocked 8 to 10 shovel types so every material came out near the same optimal load.
