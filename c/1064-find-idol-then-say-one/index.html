[Use when]

Your good arguments keep failing on someone and you have not asked what they actually want.

[Why it matters]

The maxim is a three-step sequence: diagnose the dominant motive, name it briefly, then offer something that feeds it. He adds that the working motive is more often the lowest part of a person's nature than the highest.
