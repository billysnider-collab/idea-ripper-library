[Use when]

[Why it matters]

Gisgo planned to bring the Sicilian army home in groups, paying each group off on arrival. The government let the men pile up unpaid in the city instead, then sent them to wait at Sicca, a region taxed and conscripted for 23 years. The soldiers asked where the generals who had made them promises were. The Mercenary War followed.
