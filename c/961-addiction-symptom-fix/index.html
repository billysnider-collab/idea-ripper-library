Card #c961IR // #c961Synthesis

A quick fix for a symptom atrophies the system's own ability to solve the real problem. The relief becomes the dependency. > “Addiction is finding a quick and dirty solution to the symptom of the problem, which prevents or distracts one from the harder and longer-term task of solving the real problem.”

— In our words · Source: Thinking in Systems (Donella H. Meadows)

[Use when]

A temporary measure has become permanent and the underlying capacity is gone.

[Why it matters]

Bailouts, painkillers, the consultant who never leaves. Every shortcut that bypasses the system's own corrective loop weakens that loop until the fix is the trap.
