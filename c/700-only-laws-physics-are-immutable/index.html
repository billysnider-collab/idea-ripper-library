All requirements should be treated as recommendations, he repeatedly instructed. The only immutable ones were those decreed by the laws of physics.

— In our words · Source: Elon Musk (Walter Isaacson)

[Use when]

Blocked by "that's the requirement" -- find the human who wrote it, ask why it made sense then, and test it against physics now.

[Why it matters]

Every inherited rule is some person's past decision wearing a uniform. Physics does not negotiate, but everything else is a recommendation you can re-earn or discard -- which is why Musk demanded the name of the actual human behind every requirement.
