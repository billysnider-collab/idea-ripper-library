Treat a polite stall as the other side's move, and ask who gains from your waiting.

— In our words · Source: The Squares of the City (John Brunner)

[Use when]

A counterparty keeps pushing back "final" sign-off while a deadline that favors them gets closer.

[Why it matters]

Hakluyt meets vagueness at every practical turn and slowly realizes the delay is deliberate: it keeps the expert idle while the real game plays out. Courteous inaction is still a move.
