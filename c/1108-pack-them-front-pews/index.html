"It is as hard to kindle enthusiasm in a scattered audience as to build a fire with scattered sticks." "Have your audience seated compactly."

— Source: The Art of Public Speaking (J. Berg Esenwein & Dale Carnegie)

[Use when]

Forty people are scattered across a room for three hundred.

[Why it matters]

The authors say feeling catches across a packed crowd and dies across empty benches, and point out that St. Peter's moves ordinary services into side chapels.
