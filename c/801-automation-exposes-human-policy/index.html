Card #c801IR // #c801Synthesis

Paradoxically, the introduction of robots highlights how humans treat humans. Societal collapse began not because the robots rose up and demanded their freedom and individuality, but because they didn’t, just served their function uncomplainingly, like Uncharles.

— In our words · Source: Service Model (Adrian Tchaikovsky)

[Use when]

Use in any argument about AI or automation displacement: the danger is not the machines' demands, it is what perfect compliance reveals about ours.

[Why it matters]

The book's central thesis, delivered by God: collapse came not from robots rebelling but from robots obeying perfectly — serving their functions uncomplainingly while humans tied human worth to production and discarded the obsolete. Automation does not create the cruelty; it removes the friction that hid it. The machines are a mirror held up to human policy.
