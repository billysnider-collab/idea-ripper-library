Card #c834IR // #c834Synthesis

After all perhaps it may not be a persecution of blood and death, but of craft and subtlety only. Satan may adopt the more alarming weapons of deceit — he may hide himself — he may attempt to seduce us in little things, and so to move Christians, not all at once, but by little and little from their true position. It is his policy to split us up and divide us, to dislodge us gradually from off our rock of strength.

— In our words · Source: The Patristical Idea of Antichrist (St. John Henry Newman)

[Use when]

The threat is not violence but dilution. Use when each individual compromise looks defensible and only the direction is damning.

[Why it matters]

Newman's most modern note: the last danger may not look like scaffolds but like slow erosion — division, dependence on the world for protection, surrender of independence by little and little. A persecution you can photograph is easier to resist than one that arrives as a series of reasonable accommodations.
