"When you see someone, flash them your winningest smile, and ask them if they can text three of their friends, right then, and remind them to vote early."

— In our words · Source: This Is the Plan (Ben Wikler)

[Use when]

You have a stream of people who just did the thing you want and could ask their friends to do it too.

[Why it matters]

Polling Place Vote Tripling, tested by the group Vote Rev, catches people at peak pride, just after voting. It turns strangers into relational organizers whose texts friends will actually read, and it reaches far more people per hour than knocking on doors.
