Card #c420IR // #c420Synthesis

**WHAT HAPPENED:** "It is possible I already had some presentiment of my future." **WHAT I THINK HAPPENED:** He's opening his confession. **WHAT'S WEIRD:** The man who will later swear he never forgets anything opens his life's account with "it is possible." He isn't sure about his own foreshadowing.

— In our words · Source: The Book of the New Sun (Gene Wolfe)

[Use when]

Use when a narrator's confidence and his evidence don't match.

[Why it matters]

Wolfe tells you in sentence one that certainty is off the table, then spends four books daring you to forget he said so. Every confident claim after this sentence has to survive it.
