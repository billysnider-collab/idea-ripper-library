## Steal

Fifteen years in, the Port Security Grant Program hardened individual facilities — building "Maginot Lines," or worse, "independent pill boxes, which the Threat can bypass rather than directly confront" — instead of making the maritime transportation system resilient as a whole; the program should aim to make the system "like a block of ballistic gel; able to absorb impact and still retain its shape," kept as a discrete program with transparency and fiduciary reforms.

## Why it matters

It's the resilience-vs-hardening debate behind every infrastructure grant program — defending points fails, defending systems survives.

## Use when

Don't build walls around assets; build systems that absorb the hit — defense in depth beats a brittle perimeter.
