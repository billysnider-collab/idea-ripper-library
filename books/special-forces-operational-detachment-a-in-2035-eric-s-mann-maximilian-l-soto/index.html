Special Forces Operational Detachment-A In 2035

Eric S. Mann & Maximilian L. Soto · Thesis · 1 rips

Masters thesis — Naval Postgraduate School, 2019. Full text: https://archive.org/details/specialforcesope1094564016

Tracing three eras of SFOD-A history, the authors find capability adaptation has always been driven by political, economic, societal, and technological environmental change — never technology alone — and recommend adding an information warfare specialist to the SFOD-A to keep it relevant in great power competition.

Why it matters: It reframes special-forces relevance around influence rather than firepower — an institutional prescription that reads like the playbook for today's gray-zone competition.

The center of gravity in modern conflict is the mind of the population, not the weapons inventory.
