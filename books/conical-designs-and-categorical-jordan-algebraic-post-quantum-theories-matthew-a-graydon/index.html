Conical Designs and Categorical Jordan Algebraic Post-Quantum Theories

The geometric structures behind quantum measurement — symmetric informationally complete measurements and mutually unbiased measurements — are special cases of a broader structure, 'conical t-designs,' that charts the landscape of possible physical theories beyond quantum mechanics.
