Server-based and server-less BYOD solutions to support electronic learning

The Navy can securely deliver eLearning content to service members' personal devices through either a server-based or a server-less BYOD method — both demonstrated with working proofs of concept — creating a low-risk environment that preserves government network security while professional military education keeps moving.

— In our words · Source: Server-based and server-less BYOD solutions to support electronic learning (Joshua C. Benson & Brian R. McCarthy)

[Use when]

When security policy blocks the obvious tool, prototype two paths in parallel — one conventional, one server-less — and let feasibility testing pick the winner.

[Why it matters]

At the time, no viable in-policy solution existed anywhere in the Department of the Navy for eLearning on personal devices; the thesis supplied the baseline for secure mobile learning at scale.
