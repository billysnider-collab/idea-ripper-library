Urban development strategies for peripheral areas along metro extension towards Hingna

A metro extension through unplanned peri-urban India only pays off with a full land-use plan attached — she proposes five integrated interventions: a Transit-Activated Corridor overlay zone with revised development norms, micro-level land pooling for small plots, vertical mixed-use at stations, multimodal interchange hubs, and a green-mobility public realm.
