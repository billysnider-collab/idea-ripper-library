Urban development strategies for peripheral areas along metro extension towards Hingna

Shipra Gadge · Thesis · 1 rips

Masters thesis — Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (Nagpur), 2025. Full text: https://archive.org/details/sgng.19276-urban-development-strategies-for-peripheral-areas-along-metro-ext

A metro extension through unplanned peri-urban India only pays off with a full land-use plan attached — she proposes five integrated interventions: a Transit-Activated Corridor overlay zone with revised development norms, micro-level land pooling for small plots, vertical mixed-use at stations, multimodal interchange hubs, and a green-mobility public realm.

Why it matters: Transit without land-use planning decays into speculation, congestion, and stranded investment — the thesis is a practical checklist for making transit money actually compound.

Infrastructure multiplies only when the land rules around it are written first — steel without zoning is a stranded asset.
