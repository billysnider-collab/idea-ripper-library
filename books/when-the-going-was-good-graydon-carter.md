## Steal

With a monthly magazine, you have ten days to two weeks between the time it leaves the printer and when it appears on newsstands.

## Why it matters

Print has a built-in latency tax — the magazine is already old when it arrives.

## Use when

When comparing digital instant to physical distribution clocks.
