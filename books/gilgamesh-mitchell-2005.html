Gilgamesh — Stephen Mitchell translation

5 stealable ideas · 5 keep

Best stealables

The Copper Box Invitation

framework keep

Frame your story as a hidden treasure the reader must actively uncover, making them a participant in the discovery.

Use when: You want to create immediate immersion and a sense of personal revelation in a narrative or presentation.

Civilize Through Eros, Not Force

rule keep

To tame a wild force, use seduction and integration rather than capture and suppression.

Use when: Dealing with a disruptive or untamed element in a system, person, or market, and force has failed or would be counterproductive.

Balance Through a Double

framework keep

Create a counterpart who equals and opposes the dominant force to restore equilibrium.

Use when: A system or individual has become tyrannical or unbalanced due to unchecked power.

The Pre-emptive Attack Trap

claim keep

A pre-emptive strike to eliminate a perceived evil often backfires, leading to unforeseen disaster.

Use when: Considering a first-strike policy against a distant threat, especially when motivated by fear or ambition.

The Tavern Keeper's Carpe Diem

quote keep

When you cannot escape death, the best response is to savor life's simple pleasures.

Use when: You or someone else is paralyzed by existential dread or an impossible quest.
