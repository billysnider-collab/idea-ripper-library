Special Operations And Cyber Warfare

Cyber operations map directly onto USSOCOM's congressionally mandated core activities, and the thesis tests whether USCYBERCOM's support is sufficient for special-operations cyber requirements — or whether USSOCOM should build its own internal cyber capability, analyzed through decision theory and operational design.
