Scipio's dream — states, souls, spheres, and duty to stay embodied.

argument #303

States are what the supreme Deity finds acceptable

Politics is not a fall from heaven — lawful assemblies are the preferred earthly form, and rulers recycle to the stars.

For nothing on earth is more acceptable to that supreme Deity who reigns over the whole Universe, than those assemblages and combinations of men united by Law which we call States; the rulers and preservers whereof coming forth from this place, return thither.

Why it matters

Politics is not a fall from heaven — lawful assemblies are the preferred earthly form, and rulers recycle to the stars.

Use when: When civic order needs a cosmological warrant without a church.

mechanism #304

Soul retained in the body until commanded

Suicide/exit without command is framed as desertion of assigned human duty.

Both by thee and all pious persons, the Soul should be retained in the keeping of the body: not without His command, by whom that Soul is given to you, must it depart from mortal life, lest you should appear to be untrue to that duty to Mankind which has been assigned to you by the Deity.

Why it matters

Suicide/exit without command is framed as desertion of assigned human duty.

Use when: When a tradition forbids self-exit as betrayal of post, not as property crime.

mechanism #305

Nine spheres, outermost celestial

Cosmology as nested governance — the outermost embraces and runs the inners.

The universe consists of nine circles or rather Spheres, all connected together, one of which is celestial and the furthest off, embracing all the rest, the supreme Deity preserving and governing the others.

Why it matters

Cosmology as nested governance — the outermost embraces and runs the inners.

Use when: When modeling nested control planes (outer ring governs).

passage #306

Stars as animated globes with divine souls

Astronomy as psychology of fire-souls — orbits are living duty, not dead mechanics.

Which is called the Earth: and a Soul has been given to them from those sempiternal fires which you call Stars and Constellations; these being spherical and globular bodies, animated with divine Souls, pursue their circling orbits with marvellous celerity.

Why it matters

Astronomy as psychology of fire-souls — orbits are living duty, not dead mechanics.

Use when: When you need pre-modern cosmos-as-alive without romantic fluff.

passage #307

Particular place in Heaven for those who serve the Fatherland

Afterlife real estate is allocated by civic service — patriotism as soteriology.

For all those who have preserved, assisted, and enlarged their Fatherland, a particular place in Heaven is assigned, where the blessed enjoy everlasting life.

Why it matters

Afterlife real estate is allocated by civic service — patriotism as soteriology.

Use when: When a culture pays eternal status for state-preservation work.
