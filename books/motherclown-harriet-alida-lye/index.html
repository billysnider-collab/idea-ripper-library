definition #363

Clown is wonder and surprise

Clown is an affective state, not a costume.

Clown is your state of wonder and surprise.

Why it matters

Clown is an affective state, not a costume.

While your soul is full of mad ghosts, your clown is your true self, the chaos and the clarity.

passage #366

Paramedics in no rush

The lesson arrives as spectacle and bureaucracy — paramedics in no rush.

When she returned, rushing back from Max's after the phone call, his body was already on a stretcher, surrounded by paramedics in no rush, and in that instant, she understood: Death can happen to anyone, at any time.

Why it matters

The lesson arrives as spectacle and bureaucracy — paramedics in no rush.

Use when: Use when mortality needs a concrete scene, not a slogan.

passage #367

How unknowable someone so close can be

Intimacy does not grant transparency — faces move; motives stay opaque.

To protect you." Elisabeth watched the way her mother's face moved and she considered how unknowable a person so close to you could be.

Why it matters

Intimacy does not grant transparency — faces move; motives stay opaque.
