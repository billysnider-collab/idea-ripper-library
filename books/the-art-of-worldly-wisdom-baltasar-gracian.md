## Steal

"They will allow a man to help them but not to surpass them, and will have any advice tendered them appear like a recollection of something they have forgotten rather than as a guide to something they cannot find."

## Why it matters

Gracián says superiors will concede you luck or temper but never better judgment. Framing a correction as a reminder lets the superior keep ownership of the good sense while still taking the advice.

## Use when

You have to correct someone above you who is proud of their judgment.

## Steal

"All men are idolaters, some of fame, others of self-interest, most of pleasure. Skill consists in knowing these idols in order to bring them into play."

"First guess a man's ruling passion, appeal to it by a word, set it in motion by temptation"

## Why it matters

The maxim is a three-step sequence: diagnose the dominant motive, name it briefly, then offer something that feeds it. He adds that the working motive is more often the lowest part of a person's nature than the highest.

## Use when

Your good arguments keep failing on someone and you have not asked what they actually want.

## Steal

"To dissent from others' views is regarded as an insult, because it is their condemnation. Disgust is doubled on account of the thing blamed and of the person who praised it."

## Why it matters

Disagreeing in public offends twice: once for the thing blamed and once for the person who praised it. Gracián keeps the private judgment and voices it "before few and fit persons."

## Use when

You think a popular plan is wrong and are tempted to say so in the all-hands.

## Steal

"It lets a purpose appear in order to attract the opponent's attention, but then turns round and conquers by the unexpected."

"It lets the first impulse pass by and waits for the second, or even the third."

## Why it matters

The maxim covers both sides of a feint. The attacker shows a decoy aim. The defender treats the first move as probably false and waits for the second or third before reacting.

## Use when

An opening demand looks too obvious, or you are about to react to the other side's first move.
