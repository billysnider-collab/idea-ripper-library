Project Hail Mary

22 stealable ideas · 22 keep

Best stealables

Build for conditions you can't know yet

mechanism keep

Just like that, with minimal complication, Rocky had made a life-support system for Adrian life-forms—a system that didn’t need to know the conditions to provide in advance.

Use when: you're arguing against over-specifying requirements up front — the system that survived was the one that didn't need to know.

The deadliest failures are the silent ones

mechanism keep

What if the life-support system went offline while he was asleep and he just…never woke up?

Use when: you need a visceral image of a risk with no alarm attached — the failure mode nobody wakes up from.

Awe is a renewable motivator

mechanism keep

And gracing the center of the ceiling was my pride and joy—a huge mobile that was a model of the solar system.

Use when: you're making the case that wonder, not duty, is what keeps someone going on a hard mission.

Names carry inferences — check them

mechanism keep

Under each of those large fuel zones is a trapezoidal area labeled “Spin Drive.” I’ve never heard that term before, but since it’s in the back of the ship and has the word.

Use when: you catch yourself or someone else reasoning from a label instead of evidence.

Minority hypotheses deserve a hearing when the stakes are existential

mechanism keep

And a smaller, vocal faction suggested life might not have evolved on Earth at all, and that Astrophage and terrestrial life have a common ancestor.

Use when: a fringe theory is being dismissed socially rather than refuted evidentially.

Trust in a system is trust in its builders

mechanism keep

As hundreds of astronauts have done before, I place my faith and my life in the hands of the engineers who designed the system.

Use when: you're explaining why engineering rigor is a moral act — strangers bet their lives on your margins.

Separate systems by operating regime

mechanism keep

They have two separate circulatory systems: the “ambient” system and the “hot” system.

Use when: you're designing something that must work in two incompatible modes — don't average them, isolate them.

Maintenance needs a safe state, not just a working state

mechanism keep

But in order for his worker cells to service any part of the hot-blood system, the system has to be cooled to ambient levels.

Use when: you're planning how something gets repaired, not just how it runs.

Finished beats perfect when air is running out

mechanism keep

He pulls the completed device—which I now realize is a life-support system—onto his carapace and straps it on.

Use when: someone is polishing while the clock runs — the criterion is ‘does it keep you alive,’ not ‘is it elegant.’

Curiosity outlasts fear

mechanism keep

In any event: I am looking forward to learning all about the Astrophage life-cycle.

Use when: you want to show that the will to understand survives even the mission that may kill you — learning as defiance.

Scarcity forces general-purpose thinking

mechanism keep

Plus, he made an ad-hoc workshop in there and a life-support system out of parts from his ship.

Use when: you're making the case for spare capacity and generic parts over single-use optimization.

Every engine pays its heat bill

mechanism keep

His body has to dump all the heat in the hot circulatory system.

Use when: someone proposes a high-energy solution without saying where the waste goes.

Design reviews earn their keep on the ‘extremely unlikely’

mechanism keep

But there is a possible edge case, extremely unlikely, that a short in this system could make the IR lights turn on at full power and bypass the temperature safety interlock.

Use when: you're justifying time spent on edge cases everyone else waves away.

Name the subsystem, not just the system

mechanism keep

This is just one part of thinking-machine life-support system.

Use when: a big problem feels intractable — carve off one named part you can actually solve.

State the invariants before the features

mechanism keep

System will give power, keep Earth temperature, Earth air inside.

Use when: you're writing requirements — lead with what must never change, not what would be nice.

Keep the dangerous thing alive, but barely

mechanism keep

That reminds me: I need a life-support system for Taumoeba—something that feeds them just enough Astrophage to keep the colony alive.

Use when: you're managing a risky asset you can't kill and can't free — dose it at survival minimum.

Test in the real rig, not the clean room

mechanism keep

You tested it in that life-support system you built for it, right?

Use when: someone validated under ideal conditions and you're asking what happens in the actual environment.

Remember the overrides, not the specs

mechanism keep

I don’t remember all the details about the life-support system, but I know it has manual overpressure valves.

Use when: you're documenting a system — the next person needs to know where the manual escape hatches are.

Reserves turn panic into a waiting period

mechanism keep

After a period of initial panic, the life-support system gets the air back to normal thanks to the ship’s copious oxygen reserves.

Use when: you're arguing for margin — the difference between catastrophe and inconvenience is what's stored.

Deadlines set by physics don't negotiate

mechanism keep

Preferably before the life-support system finishes pumping out the nitrogen.

Use when: a timeline is dictated by a physical process — say so plainly instead of padding it socially.

Conventions are local — don't export them

question keep

But there’s no reason aliens would follow the righty-tighty-lefty-loosey rule, is there?

Use when: you're catching an Earth-default assumption smuggled into a universal claim.

Urgency is a function of lifespan

question keep

What if Eridians have a life-span of 2 million years and waiting a century to reply is considered polite?

Use when: two sides read the same delay completely differently — check the clocks they're each living on.
