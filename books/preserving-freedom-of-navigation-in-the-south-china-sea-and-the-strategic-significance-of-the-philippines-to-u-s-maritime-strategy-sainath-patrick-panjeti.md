## Steal

The Philippines is the linchpin of U.S. freedom-of-navigation strategy in the South China Sea — Manila is instrumental to phase-shaping, deterrence, and Seventh Fleet support in a contingency, because China's nine-dash-line incrementalism can't be answered by patrols alone.

## Why it matters

A decade on, the Philippines is again the front line of the South China Sea standoff — the thesis's partnership-building prescription is being tested in real time.

## Use when

Against salami-slicing, the counter is local partnership, not just presence — geography with an ally beats patrols alone.
