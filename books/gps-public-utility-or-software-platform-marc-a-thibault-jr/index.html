GPS: Public Utility or Software Platform

Viewing GPS as a "software platform" instead of a "public utility" changes the resilience picture: the utility lens shows the U.S. hardened GPS through system design and redundancies (more satellites than required, dispersed infrastructure, stronger signals), while the platform lens shows resilience grew further by adding navigation signals — but manufacturers, application developers, and users themselves are the binding constraint on any further resilience gains.

— In our words · Source: GPS: Public Utility or Software Platform (Marc A. Thibault Jr.)

[Use when]

When you can't measure the vulnerability directly, change the conceptual lens — two lenses can reveal what one data gap hides.

[Why it matters]

It frames the modern GPS-interference problem precisely — the signal itself is hardened, but the ecosystem built on top of it is the soft underbelly.
