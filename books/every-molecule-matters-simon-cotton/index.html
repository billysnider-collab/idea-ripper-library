Methane as the simplest organic molecule

Organic chemistry bottoms out at a four-hydrogen carbon — complexity has a floor.

mechanism #110

Flipped glucose makes cellulose unlike starch

One geometric flip changes the polymer from food-store to structural fiber.

Every other glucose molecule in the chain is flipped over, leading to 'linear' unbranched chains, which are quite different from what is found in starch or glycogen.

Why it matters

One geometric flip changes the polymer from food-store to structural fiber.

definition #111

Polypeptide — size without a precise cutoff

Scientific categories can be useful without sharp borders — the chain length band is convention.

There comes a point when the molecule is referred to as a polypeptide, though there is no precise definition of the molecular size involved; polypeptides are usually taken to be made of somewhere between 20 and 50 amino acids, all joined together by peptide links.

Why it matters

Scientific categories can be useful without sharp borders — the chain length band is convention.

Use when: When a field argues over labels that are fuzzy by design.

passage #115

Hodgkin solves B12 — first metalloenzyme structure

Size + metal made B12 a crystallography boss fight — and a first.

As an extremely large molecule (and the largest vitamin known, as well as the only one to contain a metal), it posed a challenging problem for the great crystallographer Dorothy Hodgkin, but solve it she did; published in 1956, it was the first structure of a metalloenzyme to be reported.

Why it matters

Size + metal made B12 a crystallography boss fight — and a first.
