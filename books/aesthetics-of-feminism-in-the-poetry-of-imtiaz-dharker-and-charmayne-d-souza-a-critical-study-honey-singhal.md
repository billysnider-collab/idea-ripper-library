## Steal

The defining aesthetic of feminist Indo-English poetry is the woman's sense of freedom, twofold — freedom of the body and freedom of the soul — expressed through symbolism and imagery rather than direct statement.

## Why it matters

Resistance poetry smuggles its politics in aesthetics — where direct speech is dangerous, the body and the soul get coded into images.

## Use when

Censorship-proof art hides the argument in the image — the meaning survives because it cannot be quoted against the author.
