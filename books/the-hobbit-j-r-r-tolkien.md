## Steal

Wealth locked in a vault stops being a tool and becomes a master that claims its owner's life as guard. The keeper neither uses the asset nor sleeps, yet kills over the smallest missing piece.

## Why it matters

Smaug and Thorin both die guarding metal they cannot eat. Hoarding idle assets turns your waking hours into unpaid sentry duty.

## Use when

When your team spends more hours defending legacy code or unshipped IP than building, open-source or license the dead stock immediately.
