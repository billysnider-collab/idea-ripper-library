mechanism #175

Airline failures as body blows, not Darwin wins

Market Darwinism hides the human wreckage of each 'efficient' exit — failure is socialized even when profit is not.

There is truth in that, of course, but what the market Darwinists always fail to mention is that each of these airline failures was a body blow to thousands and thousands of workers who lost their jobs.

Why it matters

Market Darwinism hides the human wreckage of each 'efficient' exit — failure is socialized even when profit is not.

Use when: Use when creative destruction talk skips the workers on the wreck.

question #174

Stand Your Ground when life is not in danger

Self-defense doctrine expands into felony-stop lethal force — the word 'defense' outruns the threat.

Did you notice that in Florida (as in most other Stand Your Ground states), you can use deadly force when your life is not in danger and to stop the commission of a felony or what you perceive to be a felony?

Why it matters

Self-defense doctrine expands into felony-stop lethal force — the word 'defense' outruns the threat.

Use when: Use when a statute is doing offense under a defense brand.
