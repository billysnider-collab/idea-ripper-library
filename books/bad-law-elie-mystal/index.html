question #173

Gamble your life on a white-run system

Due process is sold as fair procedure while the house demographics make the bet asymmetric.

How many of those people are poor and Black and eager to gamble their entire lives on getting a fair opportunity to make their case in a criminal justice system run by white folks?

Why it matters

Due process is sold as fair procedure while the house demographics make the bet asymmetric.

Use when: Use when 'just take it to trial' ignores who runs the courtroom.

question #174

Stand Your Ground when life is not in danger

Self-defense doctrine expands into felony-stop lethal force — the word 'defense' outruns the threat.

Did you notice that in Florida (as in most other Stand Your Ground states), you can use deadly force when your life is not in danger and to stop the commission of a felony or what you perceive to be a felony?

Why it matters

Self-defense doctrine expands into felony-stop lethal force — the word 'defense' outruns the threat.

Use when: Use when a statute is doing offense under a defense brand.
