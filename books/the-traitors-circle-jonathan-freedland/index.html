Secrecy is engineered by locking the audience — custody as mute button.

The Gestapo wanted all of them: that way this dawn raid would remain a secret, with all those who had witnessed it remaining safely mute in custody.

Why it matters

Secrecy is engineered by locking the audience — custody as mute button.

Plan to storm the Chancellery

Contingent coup logic — violence gated on Hitler's next move, not abstract virtue.

If Hitler did as they feared, their plan would see a group of rebel soldiers storm into the Reich Chancellery, where they would either arrest or assassinate the Führer.

Why it matters

Contingent coup logic — violence gated on Hitler's next move, not abstract virtue.

passage #181

Arrested often, still not cowed

Repeated detention fails as deterrent when the subject treats it as weather.

Even though he had been arrested frequently by the Gestapo – held for three days as early as January 1934 and held again for a month when he was linked to a failed attempt on Hitler's life in November 1938 – Metzger refused to be cowed.

Why it matters

Repeated detention fails as deterrent when the subject treats it as weather.

Use when: When repression is supposed to work by frequency and still does not.
