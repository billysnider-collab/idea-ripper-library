## Steal

Cyber operations map directly onto USSOCOM's congressionally mandated core activities, and the thesis tests whether USCYBERCOM's support is sufficient for special-operations cyber requirements — or whether USSOCOM should build its own internal cyber capability, analyzed through decision theory and operational design.

## Why it matters

It frames cyber as a domain special operators may have to fight in themselves rather than borrow — the organizational question underneath every modern campaign that pairs kinetic strikes with network effects.

## Use when

When the shared service can't meet your mission tempo, building your own capability is an organizational-design question, not just a technical one.
