Card #c316Synthesis

Imagination is a sun in the soul

The imagination is a sun in the soul of man acting in its own sphere, as the sun in our system acts on the earth.

— In our words · Source: A Short Enquiry concerning the Hermetic Art (A Lover of Philalethes (Vol. III))

[Use when]

When "just imagination" is used to dismiss a formative power.

[Why it matters]

Imagination is not fancy — it is a local star that acts on its own orbit of matter.

Card #c319Synthesis

Curiosity from manifestation to causes = Great Work instinct

The man whose curiosity carries him from the contemplation of the manifestation to the contemplation of its causes, is the man whose instincts are preparing him to undertake the Great Work.

— In our words · Source: A Short Enquiry concerning the Hermetic Art (A Lover of Philalethes (Vol. III))

[Use when]

When spotting who belongs in the deep craft by how they ask why.

[Why it matters]

The Great Work begins as a curiosity vector: effects → causes, not as a costume.

Card #c320Synthesis

Hidden Fire separates impure from pure

The Solar heat of nature in her working to produce a crop for man's needs and benefit, is also a type of the Hidden Fire by which the alchymist is able to separate the impure from the pure, and to produce the mystic gold from amongst the dross of worldly mind and common matter.

— In our words · Source: A Short Enquiry concerning the Hermetic Art (A Lover of Philalethes (Vol. III))

[Use when]

When an analogy from agriculture needs to carry into psychic or craft purification.

[Why it matters]

Ordinary sun-heat is the type; Hidden Fire is the same logic applied to mind-dross.
