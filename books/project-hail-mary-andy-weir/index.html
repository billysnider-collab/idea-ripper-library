mechanism #229

Taumoeba needs its own life-support diet

Alien biology becomes an engineering constraint: you do not 'host' a species, you budget its metabolism like fuel.

That reminds me: I need a life-support system for Taumoeba—something that feeds them just enough Astrophage to keep the colony alive.

Why it matters

Alien biology becomes an engineering constraint: you do not 'host' a species, you budget its metabolism like fuel.

Use when: Use when a sci-fi or product idea treats organisms as set dressing instead of closed loops.
