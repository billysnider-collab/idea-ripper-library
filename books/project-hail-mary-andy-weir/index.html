Taumoeba needs its own life-support diet

That reminds me: I need a life-support system for Taumoeba—something that feeds them just enough Astrophage to keep the colony alive.

— In our words · Source: Project Hail Mary (Andy Weir)

[Use when]

A sci-fi or product idea treats organisms as set dressing instead of closed loops.

[Why it matters]

Alien biology becomes an engineering constraint: you do not 'host' a species, you budget its metabolism like fuel.

Hot-blood system must cool to ambient for workers

But in order for his worker cells to service any part of the hot-blood system, the system has to be cooled to ambient levels.

— In our words · Source: Project Hail Mary (Andy Weir)

[Use when]

Explaining why alien (or industrial) bodies need scheduled cold states to be serviceable.

[Why it matters]

Two circulatory regimes force downtime — maintenance access is a temperature problem, not a loyalty problem.

Card #c232Synthesis

Edge-case short bypasses the safety interlock

But there is a possible edge case, extremely unlikely, that a short in this system could make the IR lights turn on at full power and bypass the temperature safety interlock.

— In our words · Source: Project Hail Mary (Andy Weir)

[Use when]

'we have interlocks' is offered as proof instead of failure-mode inventory.

[Why it matters]

Safety is only as strong as the obscure failure path; rare shorts that light IR at full power are the real design review.
