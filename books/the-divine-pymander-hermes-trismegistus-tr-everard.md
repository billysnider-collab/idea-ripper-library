## Steal

Then said he, "I am that Light, the Mind, thy God, who am before that Moist Nature that appeareth out of Darkness, and that Bright and Lightful Word from the Mind is the Son of God."

## Why it matters

God self-identifies as Light and Mind; the Word is not a later book but the Mind's bright issue.

## Use when

Use when you need the Hermetic stack in one sentence — Light / Mind / Word before cosmology.

## Steal

For the Mind being God, Male and Female, Life and Light, brought forth by his Word; another Mind, the Workman: Which being God of the Fire, and the Spirit, fashioned and formed seven other Governors, which in their Circles contain the Sensible World, whose Government or Disposition is called Fate or Destiny.

Demiurge is a second Mind issued by Word; the seven governors are Fate's machinery, not random stars.
