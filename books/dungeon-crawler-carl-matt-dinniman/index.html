Passages (1)

passage #252

No pulse rifle in a loot box — only from a patron

Power items are gated by patronage, not grind: the economy separates RNG candy from sponsored lethality.

You will never find a pulse rifle or automated power armor in any sort of box in the world dungeon, but it’s possible to get one from a patron.

Why it matters

Power items are gated by patronage, not grind: the economy separates RNG candy from sponsored lethality.

Use when: Use when designing (or critiquing) progression systems that hide pay-to-win as lore.

prediction #250

Unintentional exploits get patched — do not get comfortable

Creativity is applauded until it breaks the economy of the game; the house always rewrites the rules mid-run.

While we love and admire your creativity, any unintentional exploits will be patched, so if you find something that has an unintended feature, don’t get too comfortable using it.

Why it matters

Creativity is applauded until it breaks the economy of the game; the house always rewrites the rules mid-run.

Use when: Use when a platform celebrates creators while reserving the right to kill their edge.
