Mechanisms (6)

mechanism #287

Seeing, Hearing, Mind — unison is Life

Perception, Word, and Mind are not three gods — their unison *is* life.

Thus," replied he, "Understand it, That which in thee Seeth and Heareth, the Word of the Lord, and the Mind, the Father, God, Differeth not One from the Other, and the Unison of these is Life."

Why it matters

Perception, Word, and Mind are not three gods — their unison *is* life.

Use when: Use when someone splits spirit / intellect / speech into rival faculties.

Consider this also, O Son, That God hath freely bestowed upon man, above all other living things, these two, to wit, Mind and Speech, or Reason, equal to immortality.
