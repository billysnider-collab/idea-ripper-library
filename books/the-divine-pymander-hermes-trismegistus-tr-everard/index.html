Classical Hermetica in English — Mind (nous), Light, Word, and Man as image of the Father.

Card #c286Synthesis

Light, Mind, Word — the first naming

Then said he, "I am that Light, the Mind, thy God, who am before that Moist Nature that appeareth out of Darkness, and that Bright and Lightful Word from the Mind is the Son of God."

— In our words · Source: The Divine Pymander (Hermes Trismegistus (tr. Everard))

[Use when]

You need the Hermetic stack in one sentence — Light / Mind / Word before cosmology.

[Why it matters]

God self-identifies as Light and Mind; the Word is not a later book but the Mind's bright issue.

Card #c287Synthesis

Seeing, Hearing, Mind — unison is Life

Thus," replied he, "Understand it, That which in thee Seeth and Heareth, the Word of the Lord, and the Mind, the Father, God, Differeth not One from the Other, and the Unison of these is Life."

— In our words · Source: The Divine Pymander (Hermes Trismegistus (tr. Everard))

[Use when]

Someone splits spirit / intellect / speech into rival faculties.

[Why it matters]

Perception, Word, and Mind are not three gods — their unison *is* life.

Card #c288Synthesis

Mind brings forth another Mind — the Workman

For the Mind being God, Male and Female, Life and Light, brought forth by his Word; another Mind, the Workman: Which being God of the Fire, and the Spirit, fashioned and formed seven other Governors, which in their Circles contain the Sensible World, whose Government or Disposition is called Fate or Destiny.

— In our words · Source: The Divine Pymander (Hermes Trismegistus (tr. Everard))

[Use when]

Designing a cosmos where fate is engineered governance, not luck.

[Why it matters]

Demiurge is a second Mind issued by Word; the seven governors are Fate's machinery, not random stars.

Card #c289Synthesis

Word leaps into the Workman — Matter left without Reason

Straightway leaped out, or exalted itself from the downward born Elements of God, the Word of God into the clean and pure Workmanship of Nature, and was united to the Workman, Mind, for it was Consubstantial; and so the downward born Elements of Nature were left without Reason, that they might be the only Matter.

— In our words · Source: The Divine Pymander (Hermes Trismegistus (tr. Everard))

[Use when]

A system needs a clean split between form-giving intelligence and leftover material.

[Why it matters]

Matter is what remains when Word-Mind union withdraws Reason — dumb substrate by design.

Father Mind brings forth Man like himself

But the Father of all things, the Mind being Life and Light, brought forth Man, like unto himself, whom he loved as his proper Birth, for he was all beauteous, having the Image of his Father.

[Why it matters]

Card #c293Synthesis

Nesting: Soul in Body, Mind in Soul, Word in Mind, Mind in God

For the blessed God, the good Demon said or commanded the Soul to be in the Body, the Mind, in the Soul, the Word, or Speech, or Reason in the Mind, and the Mind in God, and that God is the Father of them all.

[Use when]

Modeling consciousness as layered hosting, not a flat single substance.

[Why it matters]

Card #c297Synthesis

God is Cause that Mind is — not Mind itself

God is not a Mind, but the Cause that the Mind is; not a Spirit, but the Cause that the Spirit is; not Light, but the Cause that Light is.

— In our words · Source: The Divine Pymander (Hermes Trismegistus (tr. Everard))

[Use when]

A system confuses the source with the first emanation.

[Why it matters]

Apophatic brake: even Hermetic Mind-language must not collapse God into a faculty.
