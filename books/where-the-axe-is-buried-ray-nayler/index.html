mechanism #241

Patriotic machines write the stories

State narrative is not argued — it is manufactured at source, so dissent never meets a human author to confront.

In the Federation, they had their own self-censoring, patriotic machines to write stories for their citizens.

Why it matters

State narrative is not argued — it is manufactured at source, so dissent never meets a human author to confront.

Use when: Use when propaganda is outsourced to generators that already know what not to say.

Fiction's superintelligence always plays the villain

The default AI story is moral outsourcing: we invent the worst being so we do not have to invent better governance.

In books and movies, this superintelligent creature that ended up ruling the world always turned out to be the worst of all possible beings.

Why it matters

The default AI story is moral outsourcing: we invent the worst being so we do not have to invent better governance.

Use when: Use when a pitch assumes AI-as-monster without arguing why.
