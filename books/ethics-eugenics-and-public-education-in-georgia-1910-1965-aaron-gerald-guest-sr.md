## Steal

Georgia's forced sterilization of over 4,000 people from 1939 to 1965 rested on eugenic ideology serving as an "objective" scientific justification for white supremacy and racial hierarchy — and the state's public schools were an active transmission belt, teaching eugenics to the populace.

## Why it matters

A case study in how a scientific consensus captures institutions — policy, courts, and classrooms moved together on the same "objective" premise.

## Use when

When science becomes the alibi for state power, follow the teaching — the classroom is where a contested idea becomes the next generation's common sense.
