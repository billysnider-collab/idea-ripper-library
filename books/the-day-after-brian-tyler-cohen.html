The Day After — Brian Tyler Cohen

4 stealable ideas · 4 keep

Best stealables

Democracy dies in daylight too

connection keep

They have proven that democracy can die in the full light of day just as easily as in the darkness of night.

Use when: you’re countering the ‘it can’t happen here’ reflex — the danger isn’t hidden, it’s televised.

Emergency powers were once for actual emergencies

evidence keep

Not so long ago, the emergency docket was only rarely used for cases such as death row inmates facing execution—you know, emergencies.

Use when: you’re showing how fast a norm collapsed — the ‘emergency’ docket used to mean death row, not politics.

Silence is the tell

question keep

What does it say about this Republican-led House that there’s such a deafening silence about the colossal buck raking by the Trump family?

Use when: you’re pointing at what isn’t being said — the loudest signal in a scandal is who stays quiet.

When the rules change to protect the ruler

question keep

What do you call a political system that changes its election rules just to protect its leader from investigation and accountability?

Use when: you need a one-line test for democratic backsliding — are the rules being rewritten for one person?
