## Steal

Border security infrastructure doesn't just stop crossings — it redraws who can use public space: a 15-month ethnography found the built security apparatus (fencing, checkpoints) produces social and legal processes that bar Latinx residents from parks, roads, and freeways "free of harassment or fear of deportation" through ethnic profiling.

## Why it matters

Infrastructure is policy made permanent — a checkpoint reshapes daily life for millions of legal residents long after the headline bill passes.

## Use when

Build something and you've made a decision that outlives the debate — the fence keeps governing after the vote is forgotten.
