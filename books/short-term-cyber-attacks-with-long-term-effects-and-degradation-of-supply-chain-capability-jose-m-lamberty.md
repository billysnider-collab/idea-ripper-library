## Steal

Short-term cyber-attacks on network supply chains can be camouflage — visible minor attacks may conceal a deliberate plan to destroy the supply chain's long-term ability to deliver goods during a crisis; using a life-cycle approach and quantifiable metrics, he compared short-term against long-term risk to establish that such "black swan" degradation events are real and analyzable.

## Why it matters

Incident responders who treat each breach as a one-off miss the campaign — the thesis gives a formal reason to assume repeated small hits are one operation until proven otherwise.

## Use when

Short-term noise is how long-term degradation hides — always ask what the attacker gains from the pattern, not just the hit.
