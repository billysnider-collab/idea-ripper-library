Card #c358Synthesis

Move where you do not want — skipping is impossible

It is the compulsion to move to a place where you don’t want to move, but you must, and the only way to survive would be to skip your turn, which is impossible.

— In our words · Source: Twist (Colum McCann)

[Use when]

A character (or org) is forced into action by structure, not desire.

[Why it matters]

Agency is trapped in a turn-taking machine: survival requires a move you hate, and refusal is not on the board.

Card #c359Synthesis

Tell the story to salve what you became

Maybe I tell this story to get rid of it, or to open up the silence, or to salve my own conscience, or perhaps I tell it because I am scared of what I too have become, steeped.

— In our words · Source: Twist (Colum McCann)

[Use when]

A confession is really fear of identity drift.

[Why it matters]

Narration is self-medication and self-indictment — the teller is scared of the person the silence made.

Card #c360Verbatim excerpt

Mind knows when to stop — body does not

His fist whipped through the air and his mind was precisely aware of when the body should stop, but nothing stopped, neither mind nor body.

— Source: Twist (Colum McCann)

[Use when]

Describing assault as a lag between mind-brake and muscle.

[Why it matters]

Violence as timing failure: intention hits its mark in awareness while the fist keeps going.

Card #c361Synthesis

Apartheid dismantled — if it ever was

She had grown up in a township just as apartheid was being dismantled—if it ever had been dismantled at all—and she had no doubt seen some things that she should not have seen.

— In our words · Source: Twist (Colum McCann)

[Use when]

Official transition timelines meet streets that did not transition.

[Why it matters]

Political 'end' dates are contested by lived continuity — the parenthetical is the argument.

Card #c362Synthesis

Local music as radio unchallengeable

He is aware that his radio is the one thing that could be challenged, and he plays local music on it, the lyres, the lutes, the cymbals.

— In our words · Source: Twist (Colum McCann)

[Use when]

Soft power is a playlist.

[Why it matters]

Playing local sound is a legitimacy tactic — the radio is the one asset that could be challenged, so it performs belonging.
