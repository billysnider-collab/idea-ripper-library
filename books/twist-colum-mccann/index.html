mechanism #359

Tell the story to salve what you became

Narration is self-medication and self-indictment — the teller is scared of the person the silence made.

Maybe I tell this story to get rid of it, or to open up the silence, or to salve my own conscience, or perhaps I tell it because I am scared of what I too have become, steeped.

Why it matters

Narration is self-medication and self-indictment — the teller is scared of the person the silence made.

Use when: Use when a confession is really fear of identity drift.

Move where you do not want — skipping is impossible

Agency is trapped in a turn-taking machine: survival requires a move you hate, and refusal is not on the board.

It is the compulsion to move to a place where you don’t want to move, but you must, and the only way to survive would be to skip your turn, which is impossible.

Why it matters

Agency is trapped in a turn-taking machine: survival requires a move you hate, and refusal is not on the board.

Use when: Use when a character (or org) is forced into action by structure, not desire.
