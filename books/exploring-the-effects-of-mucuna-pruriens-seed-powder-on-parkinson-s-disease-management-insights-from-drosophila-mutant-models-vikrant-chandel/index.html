Exploring the Effects of Mucuna Pruriens Seed Powder on Parkinson's Disease Management: Insights from Drosophila Mutant Models

Vikrant Chandel · Thesis · 1 rips

Masters thesis — The University of Trans-Disciplinary Health Sciences and Technology, 2023. Full text: https://archive.org/details/sgng.19495-exploring-the-effects-of-mucuna-pruriens-seed-powder-on-parkinson

Mucuna pruriens (kapikachu) seed powder shows preliminary promise against Parkinson's symptoms in a Drosophila model — standardized L-DOPA content, higher TAG levels (low TAG is tied to PD progression), and reduced relish gene expression (inflammation signaling) in PARK2-mutant flies after four weeks of dietary supplementation — but the author stresses this is preliminary and needs further study.

Why it matters: A traditional Ayurvedic remedy contains the same molecule as the frontline Parkinson's drug (L-DOPA) — the thesis tests whether the folk preparation does anything measurable in a genetic model, with an honest preliminary-only verdict.

Standardize before you claim — the thesis's first real result was just measuring what's actually in the powder, without which every observed effect is uninterpretable.
