definition #103

Brain communication as pathway network

Cognition is framed as inter-region traffic, not a single GPU in the skull.

BC refers to the intricate network of neural pathways that facilitate communication between different brain regions, essential for cognition, behavior, and perception.

Why it matters

Cognition is framed as inter-region traffic, not a single GPU in the skull.

Use when: When someone still talks about "the brain center for X" as a lone island.

contradiction #105

No physical wire between neurons

Network without continuous material link — junction, not cable.

The network of neurons builds the foundation of the entire nervous system but there is no specific physical connection between two neurons.

Why it matters

Network without continuous material link — junction, not cable.

mechanism #106

Synapses as electrical and chemical junctions

Two transmission modes in one junction type — speed and modulation share an address.

The entire neuronal network is connected by special junctions called "Synapses" and they can be both electrical and chemical, passing the signals throughout the body.

Why it matters

Two transmission modes in one junction type — speed and modulation share an address.

Use when: When a system needs both fast sync and slow chemistry at the same interface.
