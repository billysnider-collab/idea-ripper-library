## Steal

The geometric structures behind quantum measurement — symmetric informationally complete measurements and mutually unbiased measurements — are special cases of a broader structure, 'conical t-designs,' that charts the landscape of possible physical theories beyond quantum mechanics.

## Why it matters

It generalizes the math of quantum measurement — the same geometry underpins quantum measurement design and error-correction schemes, and the second half of the thesis uses it to map candidate post-quantum theories.

## Use when

When the same special cases keep recurring, look for the structure they share — generalization is the move.
