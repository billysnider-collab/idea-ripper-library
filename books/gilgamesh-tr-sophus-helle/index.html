Card #c278Synthesis

The story is the immortality

Through stories, the teller can achieve the next best thing to immortality: eternal life in literature.

— In our words · Source: Gilgamesh (tr. Sophus Helle)

[Use when]

The quest fails but the record outlives it.

[Why it matters]

Gilgamesh quests for literal immortality and loses the plant to a snake; he returns to Uruk and the epic's last gesture points at the walls. Denied eternal life, he gets eternal literature — the oldest surviving epic is about a man who fails the quest and keeps the story.
