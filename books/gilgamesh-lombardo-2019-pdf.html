20 cards · 3 keep · 0 rewrite · 2 kill · 15 ungraded

Best stealables

Walls of Uruk as Proof of Achievement

framework ungraded

Measure a life by the lasting work you can point to — walls, city, craft — not by the immortality you failed to seize.

Use when: When someone chases endless growth/immortality fantasies; redirect to durable, showable work.

Two-Thirds God, One-Third Man

claim ungraded

Hybrid nature is the problem: enough divine power to dominate, enough human mortality to panic.

Use when: When diagnosing leaders who have asymmetric power but human fear of loss.

Create the Equal Who Can Oppose You

framework ungraded

When unchecked strength wrecks the city, the fix is not more force — it is an equal counterpart who can match and restrain you.

Use when: When a founder/CEO is tyrannizing the org; design a peer who equals and opposes.

Civilize Through Encounter, Not Conquest

decision-rule ungraded

Wild strength is tamed by intimate human encounter (food, sex, clothing, language) — not by hunting it down.

Use when: When tempted to 'crush' a raw talent or rival; try immersion in human norms first.

Friendship Completes the Incomplete Self

claim ungraded

Gilgamesh and Enkidu function as complementary halves — each grows only through the other.

Use when: When building partnerships: seek complementary opposition, not clones.

Stay Awake Six Days — Fail the Test

Use when: When a plan assumes willpower can override biological limits.

The Plant of Youth, Lost to the Snake

framework ungraded

Even after finding a rejuvenation plant, a moment of carelessness (bath, snake) undoes it — renewal is fragile and non-transferable.

Use when: When a hard-won advantage is left unattended and a competitor/entropy takes it.

Return to the City — Inspect the Walls

decision-rule keep

End of quest: go home and look at what you already built. Meaning is in the city you can show a stranger.

Use when: At the end of a failed moonshot; audit existing assets instead of inventing a new chase.

Parallelism as Oral Emphasis

claim keep

Lombardo's verse leans on parallelism and repetition so the ear catches what the eye might skim — craft is a delivery system for memory.

Use when: When writing for performance, teaching, or retention rather than silent skim-reading.

Decipherment Frame: Lost Then Found

claim ungraded

Beckman's intro frames the epic as rediscovered via cuneiform decipherment — knowledge itself can die and return.

Use when: When arguing for archival work, translation, or recovery of neglected primary sources.

Flood Parallel Without Collapsing the Texts

contradiction ungraded

The Flood tablet echoes Genesis traditions but is not the same story — parallel is not identity.

Use when: When someone flattens two traditions into one to win an argument.

Quote: Man of Twin Form / Perfect Body

quote ungraded

Steal the epic's physical ideal language when you need a vivid image of engineered perfection — then subvert it with mortality.

Use when: In writing that contrasts surface perfection with inner panic.

use-when keep
