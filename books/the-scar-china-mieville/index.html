Mechanisms (3)

mechanism #256

Refuse the bargain that kills escape

Escape is the non-negotiable good — any safety priced against it is a trap, not a deal.

No,she thought fiercely, uncompromisingly.Whatever the truth, whatever the case, however hopeless the cause—I do not give up on escape.

Why it matters

Escape is the non-negotiable good — any safety priced against it is a trap, not a deal.

Use when: Use when a deal offers safety only if you stop trying to leave.

Ghosthead dig for their chances

Opportunity as geology — chances exist but must be excavated, which reframes luck as labor.

You’ve heard the refrain about the Ghosthead always ‘digging for their chances’?

Why it matters

Opportunity as geology — chances exist but must be excavated, which reframes luck as labor.

Use when: Use when a culture treats opportunity as excavation, not luck.

mechanism #259

Never let your exit ticket lie to you

Dependence on a manipulator corrupts the exit itself; the ticket out can be another cage.

Even though he might still be her best hope to get out, she could never allow that fucking pig to lie to her, to use her.

Why it matters

Dependence on a manipulator corrupts the exit itself; the ticket out can be another cage.

Use when: Use when the person who can get you out is also manipulating you.

Bellis had never seen the room above, but she knew its echoes well.

Why it matters

Power maps itself in reverberations — you can chart what you are forbidden to see by listening.
