Three qubit system for kicked top model

A three-qubit kicked-top model — a toy quantum system kicked repeatedly — can be tuned between order and chaos: symmetric vs. asymmetric qubit interactions drive the system from periodic, entanglement-free revivals to quasi-periodic dynamics where entanglement persists, demonstrating the quantum-classical correspondence between regular motion and chaos in a system small enough to fully analyze.

— In our words · Source: Three qubit system for kicked top model (Ayrisha Sanjay Sondawale)

[Use when]

Complexity doesn't need size — three qubits are enough to go from perfectly predictable to chaotic, depending only on how they interact.

[Why it matters]

Quantum chaos in tiny controllable systems is the testbed for quantum computing — the boundary between predictability and chaos is where you learn what a quantum device can actually compute before it escapes control.
