Reverse Engineering

by R. Messler

8 rips · Making things · Technology and AI

How to take mechanisms, structures, and materials apart to learn how they work.

concept #90

Reverse engineering as a design tool, not a dirty copy

RE is framed as a creative pathway with a wide outcome range, not theft with a single destination.

Reverse engineering is a powerful technique, process, method, and means for creating a design—maybe better, maybe less expensive, maybe hardly different at all, perhaps barely recognizable as a derivative of the original.

Why it matters

RE is framed as a creative pathway with a wide outcome range, not theft with a single destination.

Use when: Use when someone treats reverse engineering as only cloning or only IP theft.

definition #91

Mechanical dissection, including biology

The method is domain-agnostic: same teardown logic crosses hardware and living systems.

It is, quite simply, mechanical dissection or "teardown" of mechanical, electrical, electromechanical or mechatronic, and, occasionally, biological entities.

Why it matters

The method is domain-agnostic: same teardown logic crosses hardware and living systems.

Use when: Use when you need a one-line definition that does not stop at gadgets.

mechanism #92

Teardown after survival

Learning is timed to survival — you dissect what worked, not only what failed.

If, on the other hand, something survives creation, production, or construction (as is usually the case), engineers, not uncommonly, take it apart (at some point) in a systematic process of teardown.

Why it matters

Learning is timed to survival — you dissect what worked, not only what failed.

Use when: Use when a team only postmortems crashes and never teardowns successes.

definition #93

Product teardown = take apart to understand

Understanding is the goal; reuse or redesign is optional downstream.

Based on this, product teardown is the process of taking apart a product to understand it.

Why it matters

Understanding is the goal; reuse or redesign is optional downstream.

Use when: Use when teardown is pitched as a shortcut to a clone instead of a learning loop.

mechanism #94

Design with a known purpose, solved backward

Purpose-first design runs analysis backward from the end state — not forward from available parts.

The design is a creation with a purpose, with the purpose being known, and the design process being the pathway chosen via a backward problem-solving technique involving analysis.

Why it matters

Purpose-first design runs analysis backward from the end state — not forward from available parts.

Use when: Use when a build starts from components on hand instead of a stated purpose.

argument #95

Technology push, not market pull

Some adaptations are capability-led: the tool invents the use-case.

A new material, a new method-of-manufacture, -construction, or -processing, or a new technical capability is the driver for an adaptation, not a market pull.

Why it matters

Some adaptations are capability-led: the tool invents the use-case.

Use when: Use when a roadmap assumes every change must answer a pre-existing market demand.

definition #96

Variant design = change parameters of the existing

Most "new" products are parameter edits on a known skeleton — name that honestly.

Variant design (or modification) involves varying the parameters (size, geometry, material properties, control parameters, etc.) of certain aspects of an existing design to develop a different design.

Why it matters

Most "new" products are parameter edits on a known skeleton — name that honestly.

Use when: Use when a pitch calls a resize or material swap a clean-sheet invention.

argument #97

Outcomes over motivations

Judgment tracks results, not purity of intent — useful for ethics and for engineering retrospectives.

In a similar vein, outcomes are usually more important than motivations, whether the motivations were good but the outcome a failure, or the motivations may not have been perfectly pure or worthy but the outcome proved useful or valuable or worthy.

Why it matters

Judgment tracks results, not purity of intent — useful for ethics and for engineering retrospectives.

Use when: Use when a review is stuck arguing motives instead of what the teardown showed.
