The King in Yellow

22 stealable ideas · 11 keep · 11 rewrite

Best stealables

Fortifications become parks when wars end

mechanism keep

The new government buildings and barracks were fine bits of architecture, and the long system of stone quays that completely surrounded the island had been turned into parks.

Use when: you need the image of a city at peace — yesterday's defenses turned into today's gardens.

Order makes the bureaucrat's job easy

mechanism keep

The secretary of forestry and game preservation had a much easier time, thanks to the new system of National Mounted Police.

Use when: you're sketching a utopia's plumbing — one competent institution that quietly fixes everything downstream.

Lights out, gate closed: the day is done

mechanism keep

The lights in the barracks went out one by one, the barred gate was closed.

Use when: you need a two-beat closing image — a place shutting down for the night, orderly and final.

Utopias start by removing people

prediction keep

It is believed that the community will be benefited by the removal of such people from their midst.

Use when: you're exposing the dark footnote of a perfect society — the benefit that requires someone's removal.

Second-richest is a confession

prediction keep

He is rich—or will be—richer than any living man except myself.

Use when: a narrator accidentally reveals his own rank — bragging about someone else's wealth to hide his own.

Artists perform indifference about success

prediction keep

By the way,” he said, “I have finished pointing up that old academic ‘Ariadne,’ and I suppose it will have to go to the Salon.

Use when: you're writing the artist's shrug — pretending the Salon doesn't matter, mentioning it twice.

Horror arrives as a sound in the next room

prediction keep

Then we heard the door open and shut sharply, and a swift rush in the next room.

Use when: you need dread without a monster — a door, a rush, and everything left unseen.

What if life could begin again?

question keep

Meanwhile I said over and over to myself: how would it be when life began again for us all?

Use when: you're writing the quiet utopian hope — not a better world, but a second start for everyone.

Vanity curdles into possession

question keep

Is she some aged dame, living in memory of youthful vanities, fond, doting on you, decorating you with her intimate personal attire?

Use when: you need the portrait of love gone possessive — affection expressed as decoration and control.

Wealth reframes itself as duty

question keep

How many times must I go over the same thing and explain to you that because I have money, it is my duty to share it.

Use when: you're writing the rich man's defense — generosity performed as obligation, dodging the question of the money itself.

Is squalor the price of art?

question keep

Was this miserable bohemian existence, then, his end and aim in life?

Use when: you're interrogating the starving-artist myth — the moment a character asks if the misery was the point.

Rewrite queue

The perfect sale feels like conspiracy

passage rewrite

Never before had he sold so many flowers, never at such satisfying prices, and never, never, with such absolute unanimity of opinion with a customer.

Use when: you need commerce at its most suspicious — buyer and seller agreeing too completely.

Loneliness is the tax on integrity

passage rewrite

Oh,” she murmured, “it is hard—hard to work always—always alone, with never a friend you can have in honor.

Use when: you're writing the honest worker's lament — the cost of doing it right is doing it alone.

Curiosity gathers before trouble starts

mechanism rewrite

As I was turning carelessly away, a slight commotion in the group of curious loiterers around the gates attracted my attention.

Use when: you need the crowd scene before the event — loiterers as the early-warning system of a story.

Some things alive aren't called life

concept rewrite

You are not prepared to call it life, I suppose,” he answered.

Use when: you're writing the uncanny at its most polite — a definition of life that excludes something standing in front of you.

The pastoral, mechanized and slightly absurd

mechanism rewrite

A lawn mower, drawn by a fat white horse, clinked across the greensward, and watering-carts poured showers of spray over the asphalt drives.

Use when: you need an establishing shot with a wink — the old world kept running on horsepower, literally.

Routine continues while you wait

mechanism rewrite

Twice the mounted sentry at the gates was changed while I wandered up and down the asphalt walk.

Use when: you're writing restless waiting — the guards change twice and you're still pacing.

Lock everything, then leave anyway

mechanism rewrite

It was plain I could not stay, so I locked every door, every window, and the three front and back gates, and went away.

Use when: a character performs security theater — every lock turned, and the leaving is the real decision.

Cities have two sets of doors

mechanism rewrite

Huge gates, swung back during the day into the walls of the deep archways, close this court after midnight, and one must enter then by ringing at certain small doors on the side.

Use when: you need the day-city versus night-city image — grand gates by day, small side doors after midnight.

Stillness with a fountain in it

mechanism rewrite

I looked across the square to where the fountain was playing.

Use when: you need a calm beat in a strange story — one ordinary, playing fountain.

Ships at sea, emptiness on land

mechanism rewrite

She had, it was true, seen ships at sea from the cliffs, but as far as the eye could reach, the moors over which she galloped were destitute of any sign of human life.

Use when: you're writing isolation with a taunt — the world is out there, visible, and unreachable.

He trusted her faith until love arrived

passage rewrite

Tessie also was Catholic, and much more devout than I, so, taking it all in all, I had little fear for my pretty model until she should fall in love.

Use when: you're writing the 1890s narrator's blind spot — piety as a chastity guarantee, love as the loophole.
