Experiencing the Elements: Self-Building Through the Embodied Extension of Conceptual Metaphors in Contemporary Ritual Magic

It's an academic attempt to explain ritual magic's persistence without reducing it to fraud or faith — as a technology of the self built from metaphor made flesh.

Contemporary ritual magic works as "self-building" through the embodied extension of conceptual metaphors — the elements are cultivated as bodily, metaphorically structured experience rather than believed-in abstractions; the thesis positions the practice in a methodological middle way between debunking and supernatural belief.

Why it matters

It's an academic attempt to explain ritual magic's persistence without reducing it to fraud or faith — as a technology of the self built from metaphor made flesh.

Use when: Ask what a practice does before asking whether it's true — ritual read as embodied self-fashioning explains its endurance better than debunking or belief.
