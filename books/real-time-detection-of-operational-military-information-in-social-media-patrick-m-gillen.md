## Steal

A machine can be trained to find military members on social media using publicly released military information and ingest their postings in real time to detect possible operational military information; the thesis's closing recommendation: the Navy should stop releasing personnel information publicly merely because it's unclassified — "Information that is unclassified should not warrant a blanket public release on the Internet."

## Why it matters

The thesis proved the adversary's playbook works — automate the public trail and unclassified posts aggregate into operational intelligence; the "unclassified = publishable" reflex is, in the thesis's own word, negligent behavior.

## Use when

Unclassified is not the same as safe to publish — the aggregation is the weapon.
