mechanism #198

Successful boat as benign autocracy

Survival craft rejects democracy — inflexible rules are the kindness.

Their boat, like all successful boats, was a form of benign autocracy that depended on inflexible rules.

Why it matters

Survival craft rejects democracy — inflexible rules are the kindness.
