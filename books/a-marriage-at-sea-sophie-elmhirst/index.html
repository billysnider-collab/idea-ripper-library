A Marriage at Sea

Sophie Elmhirst · Nonfiction · 3 rips

True story of a couple alone on the ocean — boat as autocracy, hull as tempering.

Their boat, like all successful boats, was a form of benign autocracy that depended on inflexible rules.

Why it matters: Survival craft rejects democracy — inflexible rules are the kindness.

When a high-stakes team romanticizes consensus at sea (or in ops).
