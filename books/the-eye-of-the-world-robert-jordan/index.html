Mechanisms (2)

mechanism #267

Drawing more Power than you can channel

Power without capacity is injury — the limiter is the body, not the source.

Quickly he had drawn more of the One Power than he could channel unaided; his skin felt as if it were aflame.

Why it matters

Power without capacity is injury — the limiter is the body, not the source.

Use when: Use when a system lets users pull more capability than their controls can hold.

mechanism #269

Two names for one road, and that is that

Local convention is absolute inside the village — humor from outside does not rewrite the map.

Outsiders sometimes found it funny that the road had one name to the north and another to the south, but that was the way it had always been, as far as anyone in Emond's Field knew, and that was that.

Why it matters

Local convention is absolute inside the village — humor from outside does not rewrite the map.

Use when: Use when a team insists on one global label for a thing that already has two living names.

Wolves and bears are fine; do not stop the errand

Local threat hierarchy: known dangers are priced in; blocking the mission is not.

Wolves and bears were all very well, his manner said, things that any man who kept sheep must be aware of, but they had best not try to stop Tam al'Thor getting to Emond's Field.

Why it matters

Local threat hierarchy: known dangers are priced in; blocking the mission is not.

Use when: Use when someone treats routine risk as a reason to abort the actual objective.
