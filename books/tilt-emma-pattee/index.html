mechanism #353

Vacations better in the plan than the trip

Anticipation is the product; the trip is just fulfillment logistics for a feeling already consumed.

We go on vacations—drive down 101 to Bandon or go camping at Crater Lake—vacations that are always much more exciting to plan than they are to experience.

Why it matters

Anticipation is the product; the trip is just fulfillment logistics for a feeling already consumed.

Use when: Use when anticipation outruns experience and that is the point.

mechanism #354

Apologize before you correct

Unwritten social protocols govern correction — politeness is the tax on being right.

Sorry,” I say, because an absolute rule of the universe is that when you are about to correct someone, you must always apologize first.

Why it matters

Unwritten social protocols govern correction — politeness is the tax on being right.

Use when: Use when naming a social rule everyone obeys and nobody wrote down.

prediction #355

If I do not get home, you are lost too

Disaster stakes invert: the dependent's survival, not your own, is the load-bearing fear.

This is real life, and your father is lost to us now—alive or dead, I don’t know—and if I don’t get home, you will be lost to me, too.

Why it matters

Disaster stakes invert: the dependent's survival, not your own, is the load-bearing fear.

Use when: Use when disaster stakes are not your death but losing the dependent.

Design and violation coexist — the body does exactly what it was built for and it still feels wrong.

passage #357

Amiable quiet, not bored silence

Taxonomy of quiet: peace and failure sound different, and naming the difference is the relationship work.

Not tense we-can’t-fight-in-public silence, or the shame of bored we-have-nothing-left-to-say silence, but amiable quiet.

Why it matters

Taxonomy of quiet: peace and failure sound different, and naming the difference is the relationship work.

Use when: Use when distinguishing peaceful quiet from relationship failure quiet.
