To Study the Clinical and Etiological Profile of Epileptic Spasms in Children

Epileptic spasms cause severe developmental regression (73.2% of cases here) — and the leading causes are preventable perinatal injuries, not medical mysteries.

In this North Indian cohort, epileptic spasms are overwhelmingly a perinatal-injury disease — 80.5% of 41 cases traced to perinatal illness, with perinatal asphyxia the lead cause and neonatal hypoglycemic brain injury an under-recognized driver tied to low birth weight, suboptimal neonatal care, and reluctance to use alternative feeds.

Why it matters

Epileptic spasms cause severe developmental regression (73.2% of cases here) — and the leading causes are preventable perinatal injuries, not medical mysteries.

Use when: When the cause is preventable, the study is an indictment — four in five cases traced to perinatal injury means the intervention point is the delivery room, not the neurology ward.
