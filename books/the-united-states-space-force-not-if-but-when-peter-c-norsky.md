## Steal

By comparing the conditions that created the independent Air Force, the thesis argues the same contextual conditions were assembling for space — and that when they arrive, an independent Space Force (not a corps nested inside the Air Force) is the most effective organizational answer.

## Why it matters

Written in June 2016 — the Space Force stood up in December 2019, three and a half years later. This is the 'not if, but when' pre-history of a real service branch.

## Use when

Compare the conditions, not the vibes — historical analogs are how you time an institutional inevitability.
