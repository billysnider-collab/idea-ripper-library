## Steal

China has used foreign direct investment and development aid in the Philippines as economic statecraft aimed at countering U.S. presence and influence in the Asia-Pacific; the thesis tests this by applying geospatial, link, and social-network analysis to a dataset of Chinese investments in the Philippines from 2006 to 2016.

## Why it matters

It turns "economic statecraft" from a buzzword into a measurable claim — investment flows, mapped as networks, either show strategic design or they don't.

## Use when

Money leaves a map — run investment flows through network analysis and grand strategy stops being a theory and becomes a diagram.
