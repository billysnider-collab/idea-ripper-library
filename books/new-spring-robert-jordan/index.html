New Spring

Robert Jordan · Fantasy · 5 rips

Wheel of Time prequel — Aes Sedai politics and a hunt before the main saga.

It was an ancient sword, made with the One Power before the Breaking of the World, during the War of the Shadow, when the Dark One had touched the world for a time.

Why it matters: Artifact as proof of a prior Age — technology from a war that still stains the present.

Use when an object needs to carry cosmological backstory without a lecture.

Only legends remained of that Age, except perhaps for what the Aes Sedai might know, yet the blade was hard fact.

Why it matters: Knowledge is fog; the weapon is solid — epistemology through steel.

Use when lore is contested but a physical remnant settles one claim.

Aes Sedai thought far ahead, and seldom seemed to care who they used in their schemes or how.

Why it matters: Long-horizon planning without care for instruments — strategy as moral hazard.

Use when an institution's foresight is praised without asking who gets spent.

What Aes Sedai knew, they held close, and doled out by dribbles and drops when and if they chose.

Why it matters: Information control is the Tower's power — scarcity on purpose.

Use when gatekeepers drip data to keep dependency.

In any case, it was best to avoid becoming involved with Aes Sedai more than absolutely necessary.

Why it matters: Local wisdom: contact with the powerful institution is a cost, not a privilege.

Use when "getting access" to a high-status group is treated as pure upside.
