## Steal

Physiological metrics from commercial wearables can be associated with self-reported assessments to proactively assess resilience and identify service members at risk of compromised resilience — with the OURA ring's PPG sensor, HRV metrics, and sleep algorithms performing well enough to track large cohorts over time.

## Why it matters

After two decades of war, the military still lacked any tangible way to assess or train resilience; this moves the question from self-report vibes to trackable biometrics.

## Use when

If you cannot measure the outcome, instrument the proxy — physiological signals can stand in for psychological states once validated against self-reports.
