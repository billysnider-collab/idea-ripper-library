mechanism #348

Knowledge replaced by ignorance; memory by forgetting

Public amnesia as active project — forgetting is manufactured, not decayed.

Also, “knowledge” is being replaced by “ignorance,” “memory” is being replaced by “forgetting,” and nothing, no shameful action, however atrocious, remains in the public mind.

Why it matters

Public amnesia as active project — forgetting is manufactured, not decayed.

Use when: Use when public amnesia is the active project, not a side effect.

mechanism #349

Invention masquerading as memoir

Form as argument: when the memoir is invented, truth and lies stop being separable by genre.

In other words, what we are given is invention masquerading as memoir, an appropriate choice for a tale in which truth and lies are not without importance.

Why it matters

Form as argument: when the memoir is invented, truth and lies stop being separable by genre.

Use when: Use when a narrative form is itself the lie/truth argument.

mechanism #350

The stranger in the bed is himself

Identity horror needs a body on the mattress — the abstract split lands when it is physical.

M.’s heart began to pound, or at least he felt as if that was what was happening, and then a confusion gripped his mind, because the body in the bed—the stranger—was himself.

Why it matters

Identity horror needs a body on the mattress — the abstract split lands when it is physical.

Echo of life or proof of afterlife

Residue phenomena sit exactly on the glitch/evidence line, and the ambiguity is the engine.

Was it just some sort of leftover echo of life—an aberration that did not fundamentally undermine her views—or might it be proof of the reality of life after death?

Why it matters

Residue phenomena sit exactly on the glitch/evidence line, and the ambiguity is the engine.

mechanism #352

Cap keeps its cap-ness after material lift

Objects carry function-form past the body — the afterlife has a dress code.

Just as he had risen out of his body to become whatever he was now, so also his cap had been lifted out of its material self and somehow retained its cap-ness.

Why it matters

Objects carry function-form past the body — the afterlife has a dress code.

Use when: Use when an object retains function-form after leaving the body-world.
