Solid-State High Power Radio Frequency Directed Energy Systems in Support of USMC Force Protection Operations

Michael D. Simon · Thesis · 1 rips

Masters thesis — Naval Postgraduate School, 2015. Full text: https://archive.org/details/solidstatehighpo1094545944

Solid-state high-power radio frequency (HPRF) directed-energy systems emit enough power to disrupt vehicle electronic systems without costly collateral damage to people or property — a viable non-lethal addition to entry control point operations for force protection.

Why it matters: Gives commanders a middle option between waving a suspect vehicle through and shooting at it — relevant as directed-energy moves from the lab into base defense and counter-drone roles.

The non-lethal middle matters — when the only tools are lethal force or a warning, the hardest tactical decisions have no good answer.
