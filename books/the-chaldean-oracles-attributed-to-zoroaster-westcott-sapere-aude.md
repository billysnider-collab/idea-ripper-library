## Steal

It will be seen that the First Mind and the Intelligible Triad, Pater, Potentia, or Mater, and Mens, are allotted to the Intelligible World of Supramundane Light: the "First Mind" represents the archetypal intelligence as an entity in the bosom of the Paternal Depth.

## Why it matters

Nous here is archetypal entity inside the Father's depth — not a brain metaphor.

## Use when

Use when you need the Chaldean placement of Mind above the sensible world.

## Steal

As it is said, "Mind is with Him, power with them." The word "Intelligible" is used in the Platonic sense, to denote a mode of being, power or perception, transcending intellectual comprehension, i.e., wholly distinct from, and superior to, ratiocination.

## Why it matters

Intelligible ≠ intellectual: it outranks discursive reason by definition.

## Use when

Use when someone reduces "intelligible" to "what we can argue about."

## Steal

It becometh you to hasten unto the Light, and to the Rays of the Father, from whom was sent unto you a Soul (Psyche) endued with much mind (Nous).

## Why it matters

The soul arrives already loaded with nous — ascent is return along the paternal rays.

## Use when

Use when a character's vocation is framed as returning a gift already given.

## Steal

Hence the inscrutable God is called silent by the divine ones, and is said to consent with Mind, and to be known to human souls through the power of the Mind alone.

## Why it matters

Silence + Mind: the path to the inscrutable is not chatter and not sense — nous only.

## Use when

Use when theology needs a reason that silence is not emptiness.

## Steal

To this end we are commanded to learn the Intelligible which exists beyond the mind, that divine portion of the being which exists beyond Intellect: and this it is only possible to grasp with the flower of the mind.

## Why it matters

Even Mind has a beyond; the grasp-organ is the "flower" of mind — peak, not bulk cognition.

## Use when

Use when ordinary intellect is necessary but not sufficient for the real object.
