## Steal

Realism alone doesn't explain the timing of Israel's preventive strikes on Iraq's Osirak reactor (1981) and Syria's Al-Kibar site (2007) — three normative mechanisms shaped when and how they happened: a communitarian divide weakens sovereignty restraints, norms force a gradualist escalation (diplomacy to covert action) to avoid international punishment, and the attacker then legitimizes the strike as defending the non-proliferation norm.

## Why it matters

The thesis names observable precursors — communitarian rhetoric plus a gradualist diplomacy-to-covert-action ramp — that analysts can watch as indicators of an impending preventive strike. Built from process-tracing the only two executed cases of preventive nuclear counter-proliferation strikes.

## Use when

Watch the sequence, not just the strike — the road to a preventive attack runs through diplomacy, covert action, and public justification, and each step is visible.
