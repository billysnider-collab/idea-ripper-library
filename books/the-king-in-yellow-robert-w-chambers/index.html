mechanism #326

Stone quays remade into parks

Militarized waterfront becomes leisure — empire launders force into civic beauty.

The new government buildings and barracks were fine bits of architecture, and the long system of stone quays that completely surrounded the island had been turned into parks.

Why it matters

Militarized waterfront becomes leisure — empire launders force into civic beauty.

Use when: Use when reading urban 'improvements' as soft occupation.

mechanism #328

Courtyard gates close after midnight

Public space has a curfew architecture — after midnight the city is a set of side doors and bells.

Huge gates, swung back during the day into the walls of the deep archways, close this court after midnight, and one must enter then by ringing at certain small doors on the side.

Why it matters

Public space has a curfew architecture — after midnight the city is a set of side doors and bells.

Use when: Use when describing cities that perform openness only on a schedule.

Aged dame dressing you in her vanities

Patronage intimacy as costume — being decorated with someone else's past is a warning, not a compliment.

Is she some aged dame, living in memory of youthful vanities, fond, doting on you, decorating you with her intimate personal attire?

Why it matters

Patronage intimacy as costume — being decorated with someone else's past is a warning, not a compliment.

Use when: Use when a gift of status clothing feels like possession.

Never before had he sold so many flowers, never at such satisfying prices, and never, never, with such absolute unanimity of opinion with a customer.
