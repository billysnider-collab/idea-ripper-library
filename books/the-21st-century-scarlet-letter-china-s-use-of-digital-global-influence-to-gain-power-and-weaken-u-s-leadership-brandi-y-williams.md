## Steal

China's Belt and Road and Digital Silk Road influence expansion has succeeded because recipient countries — including U.S. allies — value the economic visibility more than they fear the growing dependencies, and the coming nationwide social credit system gives Beijing a tool to convert those ties into manipulation and leverage; government social-media monitoring demonstrably damages civil liberties, and where civil liberties fall, democracy falls with them.

## Why it matters

Dependency is the mechanism of influence — the thesis argues the leverage isn't the loan, it's the data and visibility ties that follow; and social-credit-style monitoring isn't an exportable abstraction, it measurably erodes the civil liberties of the populations it's aimed at.

## Use when

Influence compounds through dependency, not gifts — whoever controls the visibility infrastructure can convert economic partnership into political leverage.
