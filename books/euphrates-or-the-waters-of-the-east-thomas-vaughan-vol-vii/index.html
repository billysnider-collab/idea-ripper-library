definition #321

Element means a state of matter

False friend alert — "element" here is phase/process, not periodic-table atom.

The word "Element" as used by the learned at the period when "Euphrates" was penned, meant rather a "state" of matter, such as the hot, cold, moist and dry natures; or the solid, liquid, gaseous or ethereal conditions; or the stationary, slow, quick or instantaneous processes of change.

Why it matters

False friend alert — "element" here is phase/process, not periodic-table atom.

Use when: When a modern reader imports chemistry into early modern matter-talk.

argument #322

Seek the first mixture of elements — fresh

This Admonition; if thou would'st know Nature, take heed of Antimonie and the common metalls; seek onely that very first mixture of elements, which Nature makes in the great world; seek it I say, whilst it is fresh and new, and having found it, conceal it.
