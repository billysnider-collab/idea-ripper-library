## Steal

Deepfake detection is structurally reactive — detectors are trained on outdated generation tech; the thesis measures EfficientNet-B4/B7 in two phases (untrained on the latest deepfake datasets vs. trained on them, both benchmark-validated) to quantify how much latest-generation training data improves detection effectiveness.

## Why it matters

Deepfakes can fabricate a candidate saying things they never said; if detectors only reliably catch last year's fakes, the defense is permanently one generation behind the offense.

## Use when

A detector trained on yesterday's threat is a history lesson, not a shield — detection systems must be retrained on the newest generation of fakes or they measure the past.
