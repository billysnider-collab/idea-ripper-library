The Anxious Generation — Jonathan Haidt

5 stealable ideas · 5 keep

Best stealables

Attention is finite; the ad model wants infinite

passage keep

In an attention economy, there’s only so much attention and the advertising business model always wants more.

Use when: you’re explaining why the feed can never be calm — the business model needs more attention than exists.

What does rewired childhood do to development?

question keep

What happens to child and adolescent development when daily life—especially social life—gets radically rewired in this way?

Use when: you’re opening the central question — before the data, name what the experiment actually is.

Social death is worse than death

contradiction keep

For depressed or ostracized teens, physical death offers the end of pain, whereas social death is a living hell.

Use when: you need the starkest possible frame for teen suffering — the thing they fear most isn’t dying.

The alarm stops when the threat does

passage keep

Once the threat is over, the alarm stops ringing, stress hormones stop flowing, and the feeling of fear subsides.

Use when: you’re arguing for removing the stressor instead of managing the stress — the body already knows how to stand down.

Maybe the rise is honesty, not illness

question keep

Perhaps young people just became more willing to self-diagnose or more willing to talk honestly about their symptoms?

Use when: you’re challenging a scary trend line — the alternative reading that the data got better, not the kids worse.
