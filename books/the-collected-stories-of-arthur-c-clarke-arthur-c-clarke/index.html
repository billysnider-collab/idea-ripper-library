mechanism #235

Safety devices break the circuit that should never have been made

Override protection is the real protagonist — progress is allowed until the system that must not run is forced closed again.

But it never reached its steady state; somewhere, the overriding safety devices came into operation and the circuit that should never have been made was broken again.

Why it matters

Override protection is the real protagonist — progress is allowed until the system that must not run is forced closed again.

Use when: Use when celebrating a breakthrough that only exists because a breaker fired.

Why it matters

Possibility in principle is not deployability — brain-as-circuit dreams die on wiring, not on arrogance.
