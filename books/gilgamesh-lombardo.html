Gilgamesh — Stanley Lombardo translation

3 stealable ideas · 3 keep

Best stealables

Boatman Threshold: Pay the Price to Cross

use-when keep

Use when a goal requires a specialist gatekeeper (Urshanabi) and stone-giants/tools you must not smash.

Use when: When impatience destroys the only tools needed for the crossing.

Parallelism as Oral Emphasis

claim keep

Lombardo's verse leans on parallelism and repetition so the ear catches what the eye might skim — craft is a delivery system for memory.

Use when: When writing for performance, teaching, or retention rather than silent skim-reading.

Return to the City — Inspect the Walls

decision-rule keep

End of quest: go home and look at what you already built. Meaning is in the city you can show a stranger.

Use when: At the end of a failed moonshot; audit existing assets instead of inventing a new chase.
