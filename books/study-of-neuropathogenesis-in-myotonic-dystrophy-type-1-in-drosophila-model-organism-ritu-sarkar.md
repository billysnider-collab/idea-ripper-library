## Steal

In a fruit-fly model of myotonic dystrophy type 1, toxic CUG-repeat RNA caused progressive, targeted neurodegeneration — the mushroom body's learning/memory γ lobe fully degenerated by day 25 while neighboring lobes stayed intact, dopaminergic clusters lost about 35% of neurons (matching motor symptoms), and the brain's autophagy cleanup system broke down, pointing to impaired cellular waste clearance as a therapeutic target.

## Why it matters

DM1 was long treated as a muscle disease; this is part of its reframing as a brain disease too — and the autophagy angle generalizes beyond DM1 to Alzheimer's and Parkinson's.

## Use when

When one genetic cause produces many symptoms, the shared mechanism (here, broken cellular cleanup) is where the therapy lives.
