Ancilla Assisted Quantum Information Processing

Extra "ancilla" qubits can be exploited to completely characterize a general quantum state — and even general quantum dynamics — in a single measurement (ancilla-assisted quantum state tomography), drastically reducing the number of independent measurements versus standard tomography; also enables the noninvasive quantum measurements needed for quantum-physics experiments. Demonstrated experimentally in nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) systems.

— In our words · Source: Ancilla Assisted Quantum Information Processing (Abhishek Shukla)

[Use when]

Spare capacity is a tool — add well-placed auxiliary resources and a hard measurement problem collapses into one shot.

[Why it matters]

Quantum computers are only useful if you can verify what they're doing — one-shot state characterization attacks the calibration bottleneck behind qubit-scaling claims.
