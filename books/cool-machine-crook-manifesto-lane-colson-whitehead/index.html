mechanism #331

The system is designed that way

Failure is not drift: the design intent is extraction and exclusion, so 'fixing' it without changing ownership is cosplay.

Black people know the system doesn’t work, it never has, and it is designed that way.

Why it matters

Failure is not drift: the design intent is extraction and exclusion, so 'fixing' it without changing ownership is cosplay.

Use when: Use when someone treats institutional failure as a bug.
