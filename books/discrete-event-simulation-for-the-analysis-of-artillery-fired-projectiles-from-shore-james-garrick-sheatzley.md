## Steal

Marine Corps artillery can be modeled as an anti-access/area-denial weapon from shore — a simulation showed artillery achieves higher destruction rates in the area-denial scenario (blocking freedom of navigation) than in the anti-access scenario (stopping an amphibious convoy), with unmanned-aerial-system speed and M777A2 firing delay as the key performance factors.

## Why it matters

It quantifies an asymmetric maritime defense — cheap shore-based fires can deny movement to ships that cost billions, but only where the geography cooperates.

## Use when

Asymmetry is conditional — shore batteries beat fleets only where the strait is narrow and the routes are known.
