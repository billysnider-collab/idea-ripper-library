Rip of the week

Purpose is deduced from behavior

Thinking in Systems — Donella H. Meadows · concept

Ripped from

Don't listen to what a system says its purpose is. Watch what it does. The behavior is the purpose. > “Purposes are deduced from behavior, not from rhetoric or stated goals.” “The least obvious part of the system, its function or purpose, is often the most crucial determinant of the system's behavior”

Why it matters: Mission statements are cheap. A system's interconnections are organized around whatever it actually produces, and that's the real goal whether anyone admits it or not.

Use when

Someone's stated mission and actual output don't match. Trust the output.
