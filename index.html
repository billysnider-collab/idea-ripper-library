Idea Ripper

Ten Cool Keepers

Stealable mechanisms from sci-fi only. No self-help, no love stories. The list grows as the ripping gets better.

10 keepers · curated 2026-09-22

1Two circulatory systems: ambient and hot

Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir · mechanism

“They have two separate circulatory systems: the “ambient” system and the “hot” system.”

Use when: you need alien biology that forces engineering constraints (not just weird anatomy).

2Hot system must cool before workers can service it

Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir · mechanism

“But in order for his worker cells to service any part of the hot-blood system, the system has to be cooled to ambient levels.”

Use when: explaining maintenance windows on a live high-energy system.

3Taumoeba needs its own life-support diet

Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir · mechanism

“That reminds me: I need a life-support system for Taumoeba—something that feeds them just enough Astrophage to stay alive.”

Use when: a solution organism becomes a second payload you have to keep alive.

4Edge-case short can fake the IR reading

Project Hail Mary — Andy Weir · mechanism

“But there is a possible edge case, extremely unlikely, that a short in this system could make the IR sensors lie.”

Use when: a monitoring stack can fail into false confidence.

5Walker brain under a vestigial armored head

Where the Axe Is Buried — Ray Nayler · mechanism

“Under the armored turret of its vestigial head, the walker had a mid-generation machine-learning brain.”

Use when: obsolete bodies still ship with current ML — upgrade mismatch as plot engine.

6Scrim vs full pattern

Where the Axe Is Buried — Ray Nayler · mechanism

“But underneath the scrim is the real pattern, the full pattern, which can only be seen when the dioramaama is complete.”

Use when: a system shows a clean UI while the real control graph stays hidden.

7Machine-governed life ends in confusion

Where the Axe Is Buried — Ray Nayler · mechanism

“While the world writhes in confusion, while their automated, mechanized, machine-governed, artificial way of life ends.”

Use when: automation collapse is social weather, not a single server outage.

8Life-support cylinder replaces the body

The Collected Stories of Arthur C. Clarke — Arthur C. Clarke · mechanism

“The life-support system inside the metal cylinder that had replaced his fragile body functioned equally well in any gravity field—or none.”

Use when: embodiment is optional and the person becomes a portable habitat.

9Power elements hidden at the cold end

The Collected Stories of Arthur C. Clarke — Arthur C. Clarke · mechanism

“Though I fixed the power elements at the cold end of the system, I never really saw them; they were completely hidden in anticorrosive paint.”

Use when: critical infrastructure is designed to be invisible even to its operator.

10Translation system as elegant cage

Dungeon Crawler Carl — Matt Dinniman · mechanism

“This whole translation system is quite elegant, I must admit.”

Use when: a polished interface is the trap — elegance as soft control.
