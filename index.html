Card #c1434SynthesisThesis

Treating words as opaque symbols is the wrong assumption for morphologically complex languages. Encoding morphology into three model types — a Bayesian compound-decomposition n-gram model, a distributed model learning vector representations of morphemes, and an unsupervised model that discovers even discontiguous (Semitic-style) morphemes — improves model performance and beats the data sparsity that morphological processes create, with benefits for machine translation.

— In our words · Source: Probabilistic Modelling of Morphologically Rich Languages (Jan A. Botha)

[Use when]

Don't model the surface — learn the substructure; the regularities that fix sparsity live inside the unit everyone treats as atomic.

Card #c1433SynthesisMilitary report

The U.S.-centric counter-nuclear-smuggling network in Europe (CNS-E) is "highly decentralized and dense" — cooperation is abundant but not strong enough to ensure information actually gets shared. The fixes: strengthen existing relations instead of creating new ones, centralize capabilities and information in regional hubs, get SOCOM tied to law enforcement, and run threat assessments of European CBRN facilities.

— In our words · Source: Cooperation among nations: understanding the counter nuclear smuggling network in Europe (Counterproliferation Capstone Team)

[Use when]

Strengthen existing ties before adding new ones — in a network that must act fast, weak ties don't move information, no matter how many there are.

Card #c1432SynthesisMilitary report

A carrier strike group's deterrence value can be quantified with a Markov-game model: in the South China Sea case study (U.S. as global power, China as regional power), the two players deploy limited forces and compete for alliances among the Philippines, Vietnam, Taiwan, Singapore, Indonesia, and Malaysia — deterrence emerges from the alliance competition inside the DIME (diplomatic, information, military, economic) construct, not from hull counts alone.

— In our words · Source: Assessing the deterrence value of carrier presence against adversary aggression in a coalition environment (Roger L. Huffstetler)

[Use when]

Deterrence is a game, not a fleet count — model the alliance competition, not just the hulls, when asking what a carrier deployment is worth.

Card #c1431SynthesisMilitary report

The sponsor-affiliate (patron-proxy) relationship is won or lost on "client management": the IRGC held 35 years of influence over SCIRI, the Badr Corps, and the militias that descended from Badr in Iraqi politics by building trusted agents during a formative period of tight control and integration in the Iran-Iraq War, then placing those agents at the head of new organizations after Badr returned to Iraq.

— In our words · Source: Effective client management: maximizing the influence of external sponsors over affiliated armed groups (Anders C. Hamlin)

[Use when]

Manage the client before you arm it — lasting proxy influence comes from trusted agents planted during a period of tight control, not from the weapons supplied later.

Card #c1412SynthesisThesis

In metagenomic read classification, spaced seeds (k-mers with wildcard positions) beat traditional contiguous k-mers — but with an explicit operating envelope: the advantage holds below ~0.9 sequence identity; for very high-quality alignments (identity ~0.95+) contiguous k-mers with hit-number scoring win. Jaccard-index scoring amplifies the spaced-seed advantage for small-weight seeds.

— In our words · Source: Spaced seeds improve k-mer-based metagenomic classification (Karel Brinda)

[Use when]

Every method has an operating envelope — spaced seeds win at low identity, contiguous k-mers at high identity; the honest answer is a switch, not a champion.

Card #c1411SynthesisThesis

Literature screening plus ENVI-met microclimate simulations on a Brno case site (Římské náměstí) show targeted nature-based solutions — trees and hedges, permeable green surfaces, green walls first — can significantly mitigate the urban heat island even under challenging climatic variability, with final-design air temperatures and UTCI values compared against both current and rural baselines.

— In our words · Source: Nature based solutions for urban heat island mitigation: A design application in climates with variable precipitation regime (Rozálie Červenková)

[Use when]

Rank by effectiveness, not fashion — the cooling toolbox (trees first, then permeable surfaces, then green walls) beats picking the photogenic intervention.
